# LorePath

**See your story, zone by zone.**

LorePath draws every zone's questlines as storyline maps, in the style of attunement path views. At a glance you can see which quests you've done, which you've missed, and where the next part of each story starts.

Built for **WoW Forever**. It's lightweight: no map pins and no objective tracking, so it works alongside the default quest UI.

## Features

- **Storyline maps:** each zone's quest chains, drawn from the first quest to the last, with lines showing what unlocks what.
- **Only your quests:** quests for another faction, race or class are left out, and profession quests show only if you have that profession.
- **Clear status colours:** completed, in your log, available now, needs a higher level, needs an earlier quest, and skipped breadcrumbs.
- **Zone progress:** every zone shows how many of its quests you've done.
- **Show next quest:** one click per storyline sets the game's own waypoint on where the next quest starts and opens the map there. You can also click any quest for a waypoint to its start.
- **Quest tooltips:** level, requirements, what comes before and after, where it starts, and rewards.
- **Minimap button:** left-click to open, right-click for settings, drag to move.
- **Settings** under *Options → AddOns → LorePath*: hide finished storylines, hide standalone quests, window size, and more.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/lp` | Open or close LorePath |
| `/lp here` | Open on the zone you're in |
| `/lp config` | Open settings |
| `/lp minimap` | Show or hide the minimap button |

You can also bind a key under *Options → Key Bindings → AddOns → LorePath*.

## Beta

This is an early beta. It covers outdoor zone quests; dungeon quests and lore are planned. Storylines are currently named after their first quest.
