Data/Lore.lua (and its source, tools/lore/zones.txt) holds zone lore
summarised from articles on the Warcraft Wiki, https://warcraft.wiki.gg.
Each entry names the article it was summarised from; the article's history
page lists its authors.

Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0), and these
summaries, as adaptations of it, are released under the same licence:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Full licence text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode

The summaries were shortened and edited to fit WoW Forever's time; the
changes are in this repository's history.
