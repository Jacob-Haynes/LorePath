Data/Lore.lua (and its source, tools/lore/zones.txt) holds zone lore
summarised from articles on the Warcraft Wiki, https://warcraft.wiki.gg.
Each entry names the articles it was summarised from; each article's history
page lists its authors. tools/lore/wiki/ (in the source repository, not the
addon) holds snapshots of those articles' text, unchanged.

The summaries were shortened and edited to fit WoW Forever's time; the
changes are in this repository's history.
