# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Lore chapters:** a zone's About section now grows as you play. Under its opening lines, each chapter of the zone's story appears once you've finished the quest that reveals it, and a line tells you when there's more to uncover in the zone's storylines, without naming a quest. Tirisfal Glades has the first: Naxxramas' attack on the Undercity and the story of Bandarion Keep and its Forsaken paladins. **Show spoilers** shows every chapter.

## 1.4.0-beta1

- **About this zone:** a zone's page now opens with a few lines of its lore as it is in WoW Forever, summarised from the Warcraft Wiki, for the starting zones to begin with, Zephras Isle among them. Tirisfal Glades' tells of Bandarion Keep and the Forsaken paladins. Click its header to collapse it.

## 1.3.1

- **Show in LorePath from a quest giver:** the quest dialog a quest giver opens now has the same LorePath book button as the quest log, opening the quest it's showing in its storyline, with its details.

## 1.3.0

Everything from the 1.3.0 betas (details below), plus two more changes. The highlights:

- **Story so far:** a **Story so far** button in a storyline's header opens its story in the side panel: the text of each quest you've finished in it, in order. It follows the chain across zones, back to where it began and on past this page, and says where it goes next. Only quests you've finished, so nothing ahead is spoiled.
- **Storylines follow their chains:** parts of one chain that only meet in another zone, or that leave a page and come back, now show as one storyline, and a chain keeps its name across zones. Hover a storyline's name to see where its chain began.
- **Better storyline names:** a storyline that opens with an errand or a breadcrumb named after an NPC is named after the quest it leads to.
- **Your own version of a quest:** class quests on zone pages, and WoW Forever's starting-area chains like The Adventurer, show the version for your class and starting area, not every one.
- **Latest AllTheThings data:** 100 more quests, among them the racial mount quests, and start points for 111 quests that had none.
- **Fixes:** A Quick Craft no longer shows for the Horde, and dungeon item quests show the portal icon.

Since 1.3.0-beta6:

- **Latest AllTheThings data:** 19 more quests, 12 of them in one of WoW Forever's own dungeons, five quests now marked as breadcrumbs, and two new notes, among them how to use the disguise in Jaina's tower. Desolace's later Gelkis Clan quests are open to both factions, not just the Horde.
- **A Quick Craft is Alliance only:** the Ironforge quest no longer shows for the Horde, and for the Alliance it now comes after the Redridge quest that leads to it instead of waiting on a quest you couldn't see. Other quests that only follow one faction's quests are now marked as that faction's, which matters most when the chain crosses zones.

## 1.3.0-beta6

- **Dungeon item quests show the portal icon:** a quest started by an item that only drops inside a dungeon or raid, like An Unholy Alliance from Charlga Razorflank in Razorfen Kraul, now has the portal icon and says it's picked up inside.
- **Better names for storylines that open with a breadcrumb:** a breadcrumb named after the NPC who gives it or the one it sends you to no longer names its storyline; the quest it leads to does. Stonetalon's Ziz Fizziks is now Super Reaper 6000, and Malin's Request is Worth Its Weight in Gold. A breadcrumb with a name of its own, like Taking Back Silithus, still names its storyline.
- **One version of a starting-area chain:** WoW Forever's The Adventurer chain has a version in every starting area, and you saw each one your faction could take. Now you see your own starting area's, the same way class quests show your own version.
- **Long chains that come back are one storyline:** a chain that leaves a page for a long stretch and then comes back is now shown as one storyline, not as a separate one where it returns. Swamp of Sorrows' Uniting the Shattered Amulet and Blasted Lands' You Are Rakh'likh, Demon are now part of their chain.
- **A chain keeps its name across zones:** a storyline that carries on a chain from another zone is now named after the chain, as it is where the chain starts, instead of after its first quest on this page. The hunter chain is The Hunter's Way in the Barrens too, not Sergra Darkthorn. When a chain starts with only a breadcrumb or errand there, the storyline keeps its own name.
- **Class quests show your own version on zone pages too:** a zone's page now shows the same version of a class quest as the Class page, so a Skyborne Shaman who did Zephras Isle's Call of Fire no longer sees the Barrens chain, still to do, on The Barrens' page. The same goes for the other Shaman totem quests and other class quests with a version per city or starting area.

## 1.3.0-beta5

- **Latest AllTheThings data:** 81 more quests, among them the racial mount quests, and quest givers and starting points for 111 quests that had none, so they get map pins. Tooga's Quest now has a note on catching its follow-up quest during the escort.

## 1.3.0-beta4

- **Story so far reads on, too:** finished quests on other pages that come after a storyline are now part of its story, so Hillsbrad's Journey to Tarren Mill story carries on into Thunder Bluff. When you've finished a page's part and the chain goes on somewhere you haven't been yet, it says where.

## 1.3.0-beta3

- **Story so far follows chains across zones:** a storyline's story now starts where its chain began, and includes the steps in between, even on other pages. Thunder Bluff's Forsaken Aid story starts with The Spirits of Stonetalon in Stonetalon Mountains and goes through Hillsbrad Foothills before The Flying Machine Airport. Each chapter on another page says which zone it's in.

## 1.3.0-beta2

- **Story so far:** a **Story so far** button in a storyline's header opens its story in the side panel: the text of each quest you've finished in it, in order, from what the quest giver asked to what they said when you turned it in. Only quests you've finished, so nothing ahead is spoiled.

## 1.3.0-beta1

- **Chains split across a page are joined:** parts of one chain that only meet in another zone now show as one storyline, in order. Thunder Bluff's Forsaken Aid and The Flying Machine Airport, either side of Hillsbrad, are one storyline instead of two.
- **Better storyline names:** a storyline that opens with an errand (*Speak with Ruga*, *Report to Kadrak*) is named after the quest it leads to.
- **Where a chain began:** hover a storyline's name to see which quest it starts with and, when its chain began in another zone, where (*Part of a chain that starts with The Spirits of Stonetalon, in Stonetalon Mountains*).
- **Chief Engineer Scooty:** its note now says Sovik only offers it after you pick his conversation option.

## 1.2.1

Easier sharing, and more shared quest text.

- **Share without a GitHub account:** paste your `/lp share` code into the new [share page](https://lorepath-share.jacobhaynes49.workers.dev) and press Send. The share window has a box to copy its address from. A GitHub issue still works too.
- **Shared quest text no longer carries your class and race:** where quest text talks to you ("Well done, mage!"), saved and shared text puts `$C` and `$R` back, so it reads right for everyone, and on your other characters. Text LorePath already has is left out of share codes, which keeps them shorter and keeps out gendered wording. Where WoW Forever has reworded a quest, its wording is still shared.
- **More community quest text:** 38 more quests from Ashenvale, Stonetalon Mountains, the Barrens and Blackfathom Deeps, shared by a player, and missing progress text, completion text and rewards for 9 more. 288 quests in all.

## 1.2.0

Everything from the 1.2.0 betas (details below), plus two more changes. The highlights:

- **Quests on the world map:** a "!" where each quest starts, gold for your level, grey for low-level and red for too high, each with its own filter in the world map's menu. Hover for the tooltip, click for a waypoint, Shift+click to open it in LorePath.
- **Attunements:** a page for each key chain (Onyxia's Lair, Upper Blackrock Spire, Scholomance and Shadowforge City), with your faction's quests as one storyline and a tick once you have the key.
- **Storylines that cross zones:** faded tiles at a storyline's ends show where it comes from and where it goes on (*< From Elwynn Forest*, *Continues in Westfall >*), with that zone's icon. Click one to go there.
- **Long storylines wrap** into bands instead of running off the right of the window, which is a little wider too.
- **Where a quest's item comes from:** item-started quests say what drops or holds the item, and where.
- **Share where quests start**, so WoW Forever's own quests get start points and **Show on map**.
- **Tidier zone list and settings:** progress colours as a smooth gradient, finished sections start collapsed, Tier 0.5 on your Class page, dungeons in level order, Timbermaw Hold's quests in their zones, and a settings page grouped under **Window**, **Quests** and **World map**.
- **Fixes:** an error at some quest givers, quests that showed twice, and close buttons that didn't work in combat.

Since 1.2.0-beta5:

- **Dungeons in level order:** the **Dungeons** heading in the zone list goes from the lowest-level dungeon to the highest, using WoW Forever's level ranges, instead of alphabetically. Raids and battlegrounds stay alphabetical.
- **Zone icons on cross-zone tiles:** the faded tiles at a storyline's ends (*< From Elwynn Forest*, *Continues in Westfall >*) show that zone's icon, the same one as in the zone list, so you can see at a glance where the story goes.

## 1.2.0-beta5

- **Four quests across before wrapping:** the window is a little wider (1140 instead of 1120), so a long storyline shows four quests side by side before it wraps, instead of three.
- **Close buttons work in combat:** the close buttons on LorePath's window, the quest details and the share window did nothing during combat. They now close them, the same as Escape.

## 1.2.0-beta4

- **Fixed an error at quest givers:** "Something went wrong (QUEST_DETAIL) ... a secret string value" when a quest giver offered you a quest. The game keeps some quest givers' details secret from addons; LorePath now skips those instead of failing, so it still saves the quest's text (just not where it started).

## 1.2.0-beta3

- **Quests shown twice, fixed:** Amongst the Ruins (Blackfathom Deeps) is in the quest data twice, under two IDs, so it showed as available even while it was in your log. And Horde Shamans saw Call of Air twice, from Orgrimmar and from Thunder Bluff. Now each shows once: the one you have or have done, else the one nearest your race's home.
- **Fewer map pins by default:** only **Quests at your level** is on; low-level quests and quests above your level are off until you tick them in the map's filter menu or the settings. If you already have beta2, your current choices are kept.
- **Tidier settings page:** it scrolls instead of running off the bottom, and its options are grouped under **Window**, **Quests** and **World map**, with the three map filters indented under **Show quests on the map**.
- **Progress colours as a gradient:** zone list names you've started go smoothly from red through orange and yellow to green as you finish their quests, replacing beta2's white-to-green. Finished ones are fully green, with a tick.
- **Tier 0.5 on the Class page:** the Dungeon Set 2 upgrade chain no longer has its own "Tier 0.5 Sets" page under Dungeons. It's on your Class page instead, whole: your class's quests and the steps every class shares in between, which the Class page used to leave out.
- **Storylines that cross zones:** a storyline that starts from a quest in another zone, or goes on into one, now shows those quests too, as faded tiles with a blue border joined to the graph: *< From Elwynn Forest* before the quest they unlock, *Continues in Westfall >* after the one that leads there. Hover one to see where it is; click it to go there. They don't count towards the storyline's progress. About a third of storylines cross a zone somewhere.
- **Long storylines wrap:** a storyline wider than the window carries on in another band below, like text, instead of running off to the right, with its lines following it round. The longest chains (Zephras Isle's, and the Tier 0.5 chain on the Class page) now read without scrolling sideways.
- **No more "Kalimdor" page:** the 15 Timbermaw Hold reputation quests (Timbermaw Ally, Deadwood of the North and the rest) were on a page of their own named after the continent, because the cave they're from isn't a zone. They're now in the zones they start in: Felwood, or Winterspring for the Winterfall quests.

## 1.2.0-beta2

- **Attunements:** a new **Attunements** heading in the zone list has a page for each key chain: **Onyxia's Lair** (the Drakefire Amulet), **Upper Blackrock Spire** (the Seal of Ascension), **Scholomance** (the Skeleton Key) and **Shadowforge City** (the Shadowforge Key). Each page shows your faction's chain as one storyline, even where it crosses zones and dungeons, and ticks off the key once it's in your bags or bank. Its progress counts the quests and the key.
- **World map pins, reworked:** they're on by default, and the tick box on the map is gone: **LorePath quests** in the world map's filter menu has three filters instead, also in the settings: **Quests at your level** (a gold "!"), **Low-level quests** (grey) and **Quests above your level** (red), all on. Shift+click a pin to open its quest in LorePath. The "quests in your log" option is gone.
- **Calmer progress colours:** zone list names you've started now fade from white towards green as you finish their quests, instead of going from red through orange and gold. Only finished ones are fully green, with a tick.

## 1.2.0-beta1

- **Quests on the world map (optional):** turn on **LorePath quests** on the world map, or **Show quests on the world map** in the settings, to see a "!" where each quest you can pick up starts, coloured by difficulty. Quests at the same spot share one pin; hover for the quest's tooltip, click for a waypoint. Off by default, with two more settings for quests above your level and quests in your log.
- **Where a quest's item comes from:** a quest that starts from an item says so, in its tooltip and details, with what drops or holds the item and where (for example *Dropped by Blackfathom Tide Priestess (33%), in Blackfathom Deeps*). Click an outdoor source in the details to set a waypoint at it. Sources are known for 56 of the 127 item-started quests, from pfQuest's data.
- **Share where quests start:** when a quest giver, object or item offers you a quest, LorePath saves who offered it and where you were, and `/lp share` includes it for quests LorePath has no start for, mostly WoW Forever's own. Once shared, those quests get a start point, quest giver and **Show on map**, and item-started quests with no known source say where players picked them up (for example *Picked up from an item around Ashenvale (34.5, 48.2)*).
- **Finished sections start collapsed:** a zone list section where you've done every quest (Class, a continent, Dungeons and so on) starts collapsed, like a finished storyline. Open it and it stays open; the section holding the page you're on is never hidden.

## 1.1.0

A big update: everything from the 1.1.0 betas (details below), plus two more changes. The highlights:

- **Quest search:** a search box above the zone list finds any quest by name or ID.
- **Class and Professions pages:** every quest for your class, and for each of your professions, in one place, with chains that cross zones shown whole.
- **A richer zone list:** icons, progress colours from red to green, WoW Forever's level ranges, a **Battlegrounds** section, and Scarlet Monastery's wings on their own pages.
- **Quest log, both ways:** open a quest you're on in the game's quest log, or open the quest log's selected quest in LorePath.
- **Good to know:** AllTheThings' notes on quests, such as where an item drops, in tooltips and details.
- **Clearer quest icons:** a portal for quests picked up inside dungeons and raids, and class, profession and starting-item icons. **Shift+click** item rewards to link them in chat.
- **Drag from anywhere** on the graph to pan, even from a quest.
- **Share your quest text** (`/lp share`, or the settings page) so everyone gets the story of WoW Forever's own quests.
- **More accurate quests:** quests with Alliance and Horde versions, race-specific versions (like the Shaman totem quests) and dead-end breadcrumbs are all handled.

Since 1.1.0-beta3:

- **Level ranges always visible:** the range now has its own column before the progress count, so a long name (Stonetalon Mountains) is shortened instead of hiding the range. Hover a zone for its full name, range and progress.
- **More quest icons:** profession quests show the profession's icon, and quests started by an item (like a dropped note) show that item's icon. A quest picked up inside a dungeon or raid shows its portal first, even a class quest.

## 1.1.0-beta3

- **Level ranges:** zones, dungeons and raids in the zone list show WoW Forever's suggested level range after their name (for example *Ragefire Chasm 13-18*, *Hillsbrad Foothills 18-34*), and so does the line above the graph. LorePath reads them from the game's Looking for Group list, so they follow the game.
- **Battlegrounds:** Alterac Valley, Arathi Basin, Warsong Gulch and WoW Forever's own battleground have pages under a new **Battlegrounds** heading, with their 140 quests and level ranges.
- **Scarlet Monastery wings:** the Graveyard and Library have their own pages, each with its level range; quests that span the monastery stay on its page.
- **Good to know:** a quest's tooltip and details show AllTheThings' note about it, when there is one, for 89 quests. For example: the item that starts *Allegiance to the Old Gods* only drops while you're on *The Essence of Aku'Mai*, and *Bodley is standing right outside the entrance to Blackrock Spire*.
- **Portal icon for quests picked up inside dungeons and raids:** 88 quests that you get from an NPC, item or object inside an instance show its entrance portal instead of the scroll, and their tooltip and details say "Picked up inside <instance>" instead of "Start location unknown".
- **Link rewards in chat:** Shift+click an item reward in a quest's details to link it in chat, or Ctrl+click to try it on.
- **Fewer slash commands:** just `/lp` (open or close), `/lp config` (settings) and `/lp share`. Everything the others did has a button or setting: the search box, the quest log's LorePath button, the settings page, and a new **Share quest text** button on it.
- **Class quests shown once, for real:** WoW Forever marks every version of a quest like Call of Earth completed when you finish one, so 1.1.0-beta2 could still show two chains (for example Durotar's next to Zephras Isle's). When several versions are marked completed, the one from your race's home now wins, and the others' steps are hidden even though they're marked completed.
- **17 quests with Alliance and Horde versions fixed:** quests like the level-50 class quests from your capital's trainers (for example 8151 for Hunters) and Tabetha's Task were missing their level, class, start point and quest giver, so some showed for every class with no start point. They now have them, with your faction's trainer, start point and earlier quest.
- **Breadcrumbs you can't follow are hidden:** a breadcrumb that only leads to quests your character can't do no longer shows (In Search of Thaelrid for the Horde).

## 1.1.0-beta2

- **Progress colours in the zone list:** zone, dungeon, raid, Class and profession names are grey until you start them, then go from red through orange (40%) and WoW's gold (80%) towards green as you finish their quests. Only finished ones are fully green.
- **One version of each quest:** quests that come in several versions, one per starting area, now show once. The Shaman totem quests (Call of Earth, and Zephras Isle's Call of Fire) appeared once per starting area on the Class page. LorePath shows the version you've done or have, else the one that starts nearest your race's home. Breadcrumbs that only lead into a hidden version are hidden too, since you couldn't complete them; every other breadcrumb stays.

## 1.1.0-beta1

- **Quest search:** a search box above the zone list finds quests by name (any words, any order) or ID. Up to 50 results replace the zone list, showing each quest's zone, status and level; click one to jump to it. Escape clears the search. `/lp search <name>` opens LorePath with the search filled in.
- **Class and Professions pages:** at the top of the zone list. **Class** shows every quest for your class from all zones, with chains that travel between zones as one storyline. **Professions** has a page per profession you have; a new **Show professions you don't have** setting lists the rest, greyed out. Tooltips on these pages say which zone each quest is in.
- **Zone list icons:** every zone, dungeon and raid in the zone list has an icon, with a tick on zones you've finished. Continent, Dungeons and Raids headings have icons too, and so does the zone name above the graph. The icons come from AllTheThings' data, with a generic icon where it has none.
- **Open in quest log:** a quest you're on has an **Open in quest log** button in its details, and Shift+clicking it in the graph does the same. It opens the game's quest log at that quest, with its live objectives. Not in combat.
- **Show in LorePath:** a LorePath book button on the game's quest log opens the selected quest in LorePath, in its storyline with its details. `/lp quest` does the same, and `/lp quest <ID>` opens any quest.
- **Drag from anywhere:** the graph pans when you drag from a quest box or a storyline header too, not only from empty space, so a busy zone is easy to move around. A press that doesn't move is still a click.
- **Share your quest text:** `/lp share` shows a code holding the quest text you've saved that LorePath doesn't have yet (titles, objectives, descriptions, progress and closing text, and rewards), with your character names replaced by `$N`. Paste it into a **Share quest text** issue on GitHub, and once it's reviewed, the next release gives everyone the story of WoW Forever's own quests.
- **Profession requirements** read "Needs Leatherworking 225" instead of "Needs skill 225".
- **10 more profession quests:** the Blacksmithing and Leatherworking specialisation quests from AllTheThings' Professions data.

## 1.0.1

- **No spoilers:** quests you haven't started show "Find what awaits you." in their details, with a **Reveal** button, instead of their objectives and story. Quests in your log show their objectives and description, but not how they end. Completed quests show everything. A new **Show spoilers** setting (and `/lp spoilers`) shows everything always.
- **Quest details:** the WarcraftDB link is now at the very bottom of the panel.

## 1.0.0

The first full release. Everything from the betas, plus:

- **WarcraftDB link:** each quest's details include its WarcraftDB address, ready to copy into a browser.
- **Reset to defaults** (on the settings page) also re-opens collapsed storylines and zone list headings, and shows the zone list again. Your saved quest text is kept.
- **Sturdier:** an error in one part of LorePath (for example, saving a quest's text) is reported once in chat and no longer stops the rest from working.
- **Rewards:** the details panel lists item rewards. Gold, XP and reputation rewards are saved as you play but not shown for now.
- **Quest text:** two community texts now read correctly for every gender and class.

## 0.1.0-beta6

- **Quest details panel:** click a quest to open a panel beside the window, showing:
  - requirements, where it starts and who gives it;
  - objectives and description, plus progress and completion text;
  - item rewards;
  - the quests before and after it.

  Click the same quest again to close it.
- **Ctrl+click** a quest to set its waypoint (a plain click used to).
- **Quest text saved as you play:** when a quest giver shows a quest, when you accept it, when you hand items in, at the turn-in screen, and everything in your log at login. Your character's name is stored as `$N`.
- **Filling gaps for quests from before you installed LorePath:**
  - community text, seeded with 250 quests, 126 of them with no other source of text (almost all WoW Forever's own);
  - the game's own quest link goal and requirements;
  - bundled vanilla quest text from pfQuest, covering 3,285 quests.
- **Recipe, mount and toy rewards** are now listed (79 quests gained rewards).
- **Repeatable quests** show as "+N repeatable" in totals, so a page of dailies no longer reads 0/0.
- **Tidier footer:** level colours and tips moved into a "?" tooltip.
