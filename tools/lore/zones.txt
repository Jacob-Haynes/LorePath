== 1429 Elwynn Forest
source: Elwynn Forest @ 6880069

Elwynn Forest is a vast woodland of stately hardwoods, the heartland of the human kingdom of Stormwind and where every human adventurer begins. Stormwind City lies to the northwest, the Redridge Mountains to the east, and Duskwood to the south, across the river.

Much of the forest burned during the First War, but its trees and glades have grown back. Marshal McBride keeps the peace in Northshire Valley, and Marshal Dughan in Goldshire.

== 1426 Dun Morogh
source: Dun Morogh @ 6731913

Dun Morogh is a range of snowy, forested mountains in western Khaz Modan, the homeland of the dwarves and gnomes. Ironforge is carved into Ironforge Mountain, and Gnomeregan lies in the foothills of Chill Breeze Valley.

Its peaks are the highest in the Eastern Kingdoms: their glaciers feed the rivers of Khaz Modan, and their rock gives the dwarves all the ore they could want. Mountains wall it in on every side, and dwarven troops watch the few passes in.

== 1438 Teldrassil
source: Teldrassil @ 6553392

Teldrassil, "Crown of the Earth", is the World Tree the night elves grew on an island off Kalimdor's northwestern coast after the Third War. Its crown holds their capital, Darnassus, and its boughs hold Rut'theran Village, Dolanaar and Shadowglen, amid twilit forests with a world of their own.

Unlike Nordrassil before it, Teldrassil was never blessed by the Dragon Aspects, which leaves it open to corruption. It is the Alliance's greatest stronghold in Kalimdor, and young night elves set out into the world from its shelter.

== 1411 Durotar
source: Durotar @ 6896451

Durotar, named after Thrall's father Durotan, lies on the eastern coast of Kalimdor, between the Barrens and Azshara. Its land is rocky, red and cracked, hot and dry, much like the orcs' lost homeland of Draenor, and dangerous creatures shelter in its crags and canyons.

Young orcs prove themselves in the Valley of Trials before facing the world beyond. Since the orcs founded Orgrimmar, Durotar has become a home for all the Horde's races, above all the Darkspear trolls of the Echo Isles.

== 1412 Mulgore
source: Mulgore @ 6884059
source: Earthen Echo @ 6911818

The green plains of Mulgore, home of the tauren, lie in the foothills of the Stonetalon Mountains, walled in by mountains on every side. Thunder Bluff towers over the northern plains, Bloodhoof Village stands at their heart, and young tauren train at Camp Narache on Red Cloud Mesa. On the Skywatcher Plateau in the northwest, Muln Earthfury and the elders of the Earthen Ring council study the relics brought to them from Kalimdor's buried ruins.

For all its peace, Mulgore has troubles: quilboar overrun Brambleblade Ravine and the sacred Red Rocks, the Venture Company mines the eastern mountains, the Grimtotem poison the wells, Windfury harpies claim the far north, and Palemane gnolls camp in the south.

== 1420 Tirisfal Glades
source: Tirisfal Glades @ 6834298
source: Bandarion Keep @ 6967624
source: Remnants of War @ 6969572
source: A Righteous Cause @ 6969987

Tirisfal Glades, "Tyr's Fall", is an eerie, wooded region on the northern coast of Lordaeron, named for the Titan Keeper Tyr. It is the land of the Forsaken, soaked through with the Lich King's plague: its sky is a gloomy, blighted green, and its trees cling to their last shreds of life.

Not long ago, the necropolis Naxxramas bore down on the Undercity. The Forsaken and the Argent Dawn drove it off at great cost, and the Cult of the Damned now hunts the shards of its shattered crystal across the glades. In the west, Forsaken paladins under Danitha Morr hold Bandarion Keep, named for Garek Bandarion, the first Forsaken to wield the Light, who fell defending the city. Many of their own people still mistrust them.

The Forsaken have older enemies here too: the Scarlet Crusade, whose Scarlet Monastery stands in the glades, and the Scourge, who haunt its derelict farms and mills.

== 2521 Zephras Isle
source: Zephras Isle @ 6972596

Zephras Isle is a flying island in Skywall, the Elemental Plane of Air, and the home of the Skyborne. The air elementals who once lived there beside them vanished years ago, and since then the magic holding the island up has been fading.

The Skyborne's other islands, Anvilas and Siroccas, have fallen silent and are collapsing, and their refugees are fleeing to Zephras. But Zephras is starting to fall apart too.
