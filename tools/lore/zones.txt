== 1429 Elwynn Forest
source: Elwynn Forest @ 6880069

Elwynn Forest is a vast woodland of stately hardwoods, the heartland of the human kingdom of Stormwind and where every human adventurer begins. Stormwind City lies to the northwest, the Redridge Mountains to the east, and Duskwood to the south, across the river.

Much of the forest burned during the First War, but its trees and glades have grown back. Marshal McBride keeps the peace in Northshire Valley, and Marshal Dughan in Goldshire.

source: Skirmish at Echo Ridge @ 6977218
source: Investigate Echo Ridge @ 6826053
source: Kobold Camp Cleanup @ 6225852

Kobolds have crept into Northshire from the woods to the north, and a band of their workers has dug into the Echo Ridge Mine. One by one they are thieves and cowards, but in numbers they are a real threat, and Marshal McBride wanted them purged before they dug in deeper. The mine is clearer now, though McBride admits there may still be kobolds in its tunnels.

source: Bounty on Garrick Padfoot @ 6879195

A new gang of thieves has appeared across the river east of the abbey, calling themselves the Defias Brotherhood and wearing bandanas. One of them, the cutthroat Garrick Padfoot, gathered a band of thugs at a shack by the vineyards and preyed on Northshire's farmers and merchants for weeks. Garrick is dead and his bounty paid, but what the Brotherhood itself is after, no one in the valley can say.

The necklace is home again, and Bernice gave away her late husband's lucky keepsake, which he had forgotten on his last campaign.

Hogger is dead, and Marshal Dughan, who had begun to think no one could do it, paid out the reward with relief.

== 1426 Dun Morogh
source: Dun Morogh @ 6731913

Dun Morogh is a range of snowy, forested mountains in western Khaz Modan, the homeland of the dwarves and gnomes. Ironforge is carved into Ironforge Mountain, and Gnomeregan lies in the foothills of Chill Breeze Valley.

Its peaks are the highest in the Eastern Kingdoms: their glaciers feed the rivers of Khaz Modan, and their rock gives the dwarves all the ore they could want. Mountains wall it in on every side, and dwarven troops watch the few passes in.

after 170 A New Threat
source: A New Threat (Dun Morogh) @ 6849070

Troggs have been bursting out of the ground all across the dwarves' lands, and Coldridge Valley is no exception: a few nights ago they overran the camp to the west. Balir Frosthammer's shorthanded people have pushed them back from the hills and the frozen lake for now. If Coldridge is any sign of what is happening elsewhere, Balir fears hard times ahead, and hopes the king and the Senate are acting.

The gnomes drove the troggs out of Gnomeregan, but then something went horribly wrong, and their home is now irradiated. Its people fled in every direction and are scattered across Dun Morogh. Felix Whindlebolt reached Anvilmar with nothing, his belongings carried off to the Frostmane trolls' camps, until a stranger brought them back.

With the journal back, Grelin judges the trolls here no great cause for worry, and readies his report for his brother.

A few days ago a shelf of rock and ice broke from the peaks, and the slide stirred up the ice elementals, making roads that are dangerous enough already worse still. The first and largest of them, which Gavin called Avala, had been shifting the weather and setting off smaller slides; it is destroyed now. Razzle is sure it will make the gnomish race as good as new.

Senator Mehr Stonehallow, of the Senate and the Explorers' League, has taken this part of the infestation as his own charge. The troggs have been thinned: a start, he says, enough perhaps for mountaineers to move in and secure the quarry so the miners can go back to work.

His frozen, mauled body was found in the hills with his journal: the great ice claw bear Mangeclaw had savaged him, and later came back to finish him off. His last wish was revenge, and Mangeclaw is dead; the pilots of the Ironforge Siege Brigade will remember the deed.

One carried a map of tunnels and underground highways, written in the Dark Irons' jagged runes, all meeting at a point beneath Ironforge itself. Farsen reads it as the plan for an incursion into the vaults under the city, and the spies as its scouts; if the dwarves hurry, they might beat the Dark Irons to their goal.

== 1438 Teldrassil
source: Teldrassil @ 6553392

Teldrassil, "Crown of the Earth", is the World Tree the night elves grew on an island off Kalimdor's northwestern coast after the Third War. Its crown holds their capital, Darnassus, and its boughs hold Rut'theran Village, Dolanaar and Shadowglen, amid twilit forests with a world of their own.

It is the Alliance's greatest stronghold in Kalimdor, and young night elves set out into the world from its shelter.

after 97977 Nature's Call
source: Nature's Call (Forever) @ 6971319
source: The Woodland Protector (Classic) @ 6177072

The dryad Tarindrella, a protector of the forests who had not been felt in Kalimdor for years, has returned to Shadowglen, and that alone means something is wrong. The Gnarlpine furbolgs, the night elves' old allies, have abandoned their camps in the glen, and the totems they left behind tell why: they began to dream of being wiped out by a great shadow, until the fear became rage and the shadow took the shape of their elven friends. Tarindrella hopes their plight will help explain what is happening on Teldrassil.

In the Ban'ethil Barrow Den, Gnarlpine shaman found a way to tear a sleeping druid's spirit from his body. Oben Rageclaw was trapped in the Emerald Dream while the furbolgs sent his empty body against anyone exploring the den. The only way to free him was to kill that body and use the shamans' charm on it; now his spirit is free of them, to rest in the Dream for good.

== 1411 Durotar
source: Durotar @ 6896451

Durotar, named after Thrall's father Durotan, lies on the eastern coast of Kalimdor, between the Barrens and Azshara. Its land is rocky, red and cracked, hot and dry, much like the orcs' lost homeland of Draenor, and dangerous creatures shelter in its crags and canyons.

Young orcs prove themselves in the Valley of Trials before facing the world beyond. Since the orcs founded Orgrimmar, Durotar has become a home for all the Horde's races, above all the Darkspear trolls of the Echo Isles.

after 794 Burning Blade Medallion

The trials of the Valley were meant to be the first test of a young warrior, but a taint has crept in. The Burning Blade, a cult that worships demons, has made a coven in a cave in the valley's hills, and its vile familiars spilled out to cause havoc. The medallion of power hidden in the coven is out of the cult's hands now, but Zureetha Fargaze fears the Burning Blade has greater aims in Durotar.

Years ago, Admiral Proudmoore led the humans of Kul Tiras onto Durotar's shores, breaking the pact Thrall had made with Jaina Proudmoore to defeat Archimonde. The Horde threw them back and Tiragarde Keep fell, but the Admiral's reserves under Lieutenant Benedict retook its ruins. Benedict is dead now, and many of his sailors and marines with him.

Those plans are destroyed and the invasion stopped before it began, though Lar Prowltusk is sure there will be blood spilled before these troubled times are through.

== 1412 Mulgore
source: Mulgore @ 6884059
source: Mulgore (Classic) @ 5787542
source: Earthen Echo @ 6911818

The green plains of Mulgore, home of the tauren, lie in the foothills of the Stonetalon Mountains, walled in by mountains on every side. Thunder Bluff towers over the northern plains, Bloodhoof Village stands at their heart, and young tauren train at Camp Narache on Red Cloud Mesa. On the Skywatcher Plateau in the northwest, Muln Earthfury and the elders of the Earthen Ring council study the relics brought to them from Kalimdor's buried ruins.

For all its peace, Mulgore has troubles: quilboar overrun Brambleblade Ravine and the sacred Red Rocks, the goblins of the Venture Company mine the eastern mountains, dwarves dig at the Bael'dun Digsite, Windfury harpies claim the far north, and Palemane gnolls camp in the south.

== 1420 Tirisfal Glades
source: Tirisfal Glades @ 6834298
source: Bandarion Keep @ 6967624

Tirisfal Glades, "Tyr's Fall", is an eerie, wooded region on the northern coast of Lordaeron, named for the Titan Keeper Tyr. It is the land of the Forsaken, soaked through with the Lich King's plague: its sky is a gloomy, blighted green, and its trees cling to their last shreds of life.

The Forsaken share it with old enemies: the Scarlet Crusade, whose Scarlet Monastery stands in the glades, and the Scourge, who haunt its derelict farms and mills. In the west, on the bluffs by the Whispering Forest, a new order of Forsaken paladins holds Bandarion Keep.

The Mindless Ones who overran the north of Deathknell were once their brothers and sisters, but they are the Lich King's slaves still and will never join the Forsaken, so they must be destroyed.
