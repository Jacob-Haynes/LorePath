== Objectives ==
Bring the Ring of Zoram to Talen near the Zoram Strand.

 [Ring of Zoram]


== Description ==
I fear the ancient statuette's secrets will remain forever hidden unless you find the key to its ancient lock.
That key is a ring, the Ring of Zoram. It belonged to the rulers of this city before the waves of the Great Sundering drowned it.
For years the ring was lost, but the naga have recently found it.
Their leader, Ruuzel, dwells on an isle on the northern edge of Zoram.  Please, <name>, retrieve the ring for me!


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

2550 XP (or 7 20 at level 70)
350 reputation with Darnassus
 [Robes of Antiquity]


== Progress ==
It is said that the Ring of Zoram is the key to any lock within the city. Do you have it, <name>?


== Completion ==
You found the ring! Thank you, <name>. Please take this as payment, and know that your deeds today will remain forever in my memory.


== Notes ==
Ruuzel can be found on the big island to the north, self-evident on your map.  Ruuzel is not elite, but does have two naga followers.
Lady Vespia (a rare spawn) has also been reported to drop the ring.
