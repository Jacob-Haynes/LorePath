== Objectives ==
Bring word of the Al'Aketh to Rorian the Dayseeker in Thendal Grove.


== Description ==
You've done enough here, <class>. I'll stay and clean up any stragglers down there.
Head back to the Village and tell Rorian about the cult's presence. He will not be pleased.


== Rewards ==
You will be able to choose one of these rewards:

 [Windblown Short Cloak]
 [Skychain Belt]
You will also receive:

40 XP


== Completion ==
This is most troubling, but unfortunately unsurprising. We've been aware of the cult's movements for some time, but we've been content to watch from afar. Their agitation of the wind spirits near the standing stones is a clear escalation on their part.
You've performed a difficult but necessary service for Thendal Grove, <name>. Thank you.
