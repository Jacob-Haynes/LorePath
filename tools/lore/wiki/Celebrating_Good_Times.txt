== Description ==
Seek out Major Mattingly in the Valley of Heroes, <name>. He will be expecting you...


== Rewards ==
You will be able to choose one of these rewards:

 [Dragonslayer's Signet]
 [Onyxia Tooth Pendant]
 [Onyxia Blood Talisman]
You will also receive:

9950 XP (or 87 at max level)
+125 reputation with Darnassus
+125 reputation with Exodar
+125 reputation with Gnomeregan
+125 reputation with Ironforge
+125 reputation with Stormwind


== Completion ==
Few are graced with such ceremony, <name>. Please, choose your reward so that we may begin the celebration.


== Notes ==
Upon completion
Major Mattingly yells: Citizens and allies of Stormwind, on this day, history has been made. <Name> has laid waste to that which had attempted to usurp the rule of the kingdom. Gather round and join me in honoring our heroes.
Major Mattingly yells: Behold the might of the Alliance!  The dread lady, Onyxia, hangs from the arches! Let the rallying cry of the dragon slayer lift your spirits!
