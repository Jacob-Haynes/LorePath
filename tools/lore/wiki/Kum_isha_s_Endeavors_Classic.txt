== Objectives ==
Find and return a Flawless Draenethyst Sphere to Kum'isha the Collector.

 [Flawless Draenethyst Sphere]


== Description ==
Even though the portal conjuration was a failure, I remain hopeful that my next attempt will be successful.
The hope of the Draenei is as fervent as your greed for trinkets, <race>. Go back out into these cursed lands and recover another flawless draenethyst sphere.
Of course, the choice to take on this task is completely left to you *grin*.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

 [Emerald Encrusted Chest]


== Progress ==
Have you found another flawless draenethyst sphere?


== Completion ==
Simply amazing. I had doubted that you could find another sphere, yet here it is, pulsating in my hands.
Well done, <name>!
Should you find anymore--and I believe this to be an impossibility--return them to me and I shall reward you again.
