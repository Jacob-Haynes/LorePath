Feathermoon Stronghold, also known as New Feathermoon, is a kaldorei port located on the Forgotten Coast in Feralas. It was built after the Shattering as a replacement for the original, now destroyed stronghold located on Sardor Isle, which has been renamed the Ruins of Feathermoon. The fortress has a moonwell within and is under the command of General Shandris Feathermoon.
