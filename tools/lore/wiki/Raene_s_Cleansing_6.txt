This quest is part of the Raene's Cleansing quest chain.


== Objectives ==
Find the hidden shrine and re-enchant Dartol's Rod.

 [Reconstructed Rod] (provided)


== Description ==
Here is the completed rod, <name>. Reconstructing it was simple... it's finding the shrine that has the power to recharge it that will be the hard part.
With some focus and effort it shouldn't be too hard for someone like you, who's already accomplished so much.
Head southeast of here--there are very few paths, so be careful--and keep your eyes to the north. The shrine is hidden within the mountains that surround this glade.
Once there, place the rod within the shrine... the rest is magic. Hehe.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

1700 XP
+150 reputation with Darnassus
 [Dartol's Rod of Transformation]


== Progress ==
You can sense a subtle yet powerful force within the shrine and surrounding trees.
You begin placing the rod within the shrine and a soft thrumming noise emits from around you as the shrine begins to glow...


== Completion ==
After the glow dies, you can see the re-enchanted rod within the shrine's basin.


== Notes ==
The path to the hidden shrine starts at . The actual shrine is at coordinates . When you leave the moonwell, follow the mountain's edge to the southeast. Continue to follow the mountain and you'll eventually find the path. It will spiral up to the shrine.
