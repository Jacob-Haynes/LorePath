Mor'Ladim, formerly Morgan Ladimore, was a soldier of Grand Hamlet (now known as Darkshire) who participated in the Third War. As he went to help fight in Lordaeron, his homeland was laid waste. When he returned from the war Morgan could not find his wife Lys nor their children. He went mad, and in his madness killed several innocents. When he came to, he saw what had come to pass and committed suicide. His body was reanimated and became an undead monstrosity known as Mor'Ladim, who roamed near Raven Hill and killed indiscriminately.
