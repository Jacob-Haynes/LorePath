== Description ==
You have accomplished the impossible. The brood mother of the Black Dragonflight lies dead at your feet. Take her head and present it to your Warchief.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

9950 XP (or 87 at max level)
+125 reputation with Darkspear Trolls
+125 reputation with Orgrimmar
+125 reputation with Silvermoon City
+125 reputation with Thunder Bluff
+125 reputation with Undercity
