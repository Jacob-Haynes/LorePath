The Revantusk tribe are a tribe of forest trolls split into two groups: one living in Zul'Aman, and another living on the coast of the Hinterlands. After learning of Thrall's new Horde and his noble goals, the Hinterlands Revantusk trolls agreed to a pact of friendship and mutual assistance with the Horde.
Nalorakk is the main loa of the Revantusk tribe. Akil'darah and recently Shadra are two other loa who are worshiped by the tribe.
