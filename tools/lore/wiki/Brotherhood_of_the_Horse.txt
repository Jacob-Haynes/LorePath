The Brotherhood of the Horse was a group in the Kingdom of Stormwind predating the First War. Sir Anduin Lothar was a member of it—he rose to the position of Armsman to the Brotherhood after becoming a member of King Llane's Honor Guard and a knight. He has also been referred to as a "Knight errant" in the Brotherhood. It's unknown what the group's role was and whether or not it continued to exist after the First War.
