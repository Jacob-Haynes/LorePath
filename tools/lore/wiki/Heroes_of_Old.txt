This is part of the main Blasted Lands quest chain.


== Objectives ==
Speak to Corporal Thund Splithoof.


== Description ==
You have honored me with your nobility and heroism, <name>.
You have also honored them...


== Completion ==
Indeed, you have honored us all. Our thanks alone are not sufficient.


== Rewards ==
3000 XP
26 40
One towards  Loremaster of Eastern Kingdoms
One towards  3000 Quests Completed (or the earlier ones, if not completed)


== Notes ==
The corporal is directly across the road from the Fallen Hero.
He will fade away again fairly swiftly.  If he does, talk to the Fallen Hero
again and select the option  I must talk to Corporal Splithoof.


== Progression ==
This is part of the You Are Rakh'likh, Demon quest chain.
