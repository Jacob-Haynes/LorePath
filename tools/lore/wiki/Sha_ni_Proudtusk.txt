Sha'ni Proudtusk is a quest giver located atop Dreadmaul Rock in the Burning Steppes. She was the ghost of a dead jungle troll whose remains lay on an altar there. Krom'Grul tortured and killed Sha'ni, and stole her wedding nose-ring. Her husband was Thal'trak Proudtusk who she planned to live with in the Hinterlands after her last mission was done.
She has been missing since the Cataclysm, presumably have finally found the peace of death.
