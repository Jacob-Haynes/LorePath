Powderfuse Port is a goblin town on the eastern coast of the Riverglades in World of Warcraft: Forever. Melbin Powderfuse, a master goblin engineer, claims to have built the town and to own it.


== Background ==
According to Melbin Powderfuse, the site was a vacant lot before his arrival. He claims that everything in the town was built by him and belongs to him.


== Inhabitants ==
Melbin Powderfuse - master goblin engineer who claims ownership of the town and offers training.
Innkeeper Zizplink - innkeeper whose greeting welcomes travelers and comments on the ocean air and its occasionally fishy smell.
Gray Dawnbright - a patron who asks visitors to have Zizplink pour him another drink.
Jazzle Cheapshot - proprietor of an arena. When spoken to, he says no fights are taking place and asks visitors to return once he has the place running.


== Arena ==
Jazzle Cheapshot refers to the arena as his and complains that the Horde and Alliance are fighting each other in the fields instead of making money by fighting in the arena. His greeting states that it is not yet running.


== Transportation ==
Powderfuse Port has a connection by sea to Tanaris.
