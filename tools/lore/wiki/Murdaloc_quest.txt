This quest is part of the Ironband Wants You! quest chain.  This quest chain starts with  [37] Ironband Wants You!


== Objectives ==
Slay Agmond's killer, Murdaloc.Slay 12 Stonevault Bonesnappers.
Report to Prospector Ironband in Loch Modan.

Murdaloc slain
Stonevault Bonesnapper slain (12)


== Description ==
Agmond's tracks lead from the west. It is easy to tell from the bloody smears across rock and sand that the dwarf crawled some distance.
And here he died.
Upon close inspection, the word "Murdaloc" is roughly scrawled in a patch of Badlands sandstone.
Scrawled next to Agmond's dead hand.


== Rewards ==

You will also receive:

3450 XP (or 21 at level 70)
250 reputation with Ironforge
