Un'Goro Crater (/ʌnˈɡoʊɹoʊ/ uhn-GOH-roh), sometimes stylized as Un'goro Crater, and meaning "God Lands" in the Qiraji language, lies at the central part of Southern Kalimdor, between Tanaris, Silithus and Uldum. Although the area is almost completely isolated from the rest of the world and sandwiched between three deserts, the crater is filled with exotic plant life and even more exotic creatures. Ranging from peaceful earth elementals to raging devilsaurs, the locals are not to be trifled with. The zone annually hosts the Un'Goro Madness micro-event.
