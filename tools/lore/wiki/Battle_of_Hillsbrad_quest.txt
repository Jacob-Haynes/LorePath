Battle of Hillsbrad is the first quest in the major Horde quest chain of Hillsbrad Foothills. This quest involves killing the inhabitants of Hillsbrad Fields.


== Objectives ==
Kill 6 Hillsbrad Farmhands, 6 Hillsbrad Farmers, Farmer Ray and Farmer Getz and report back to Darthalia in Tarren Mill.

Hillsbrad Farmers slain (6)
Hillsbrad Farmhands slain (6)
Farmer Ray slain
Farmer Getz slain


== Description ==
Stand at attention while addressing me, <class>.
I am under direct command of Varimathras. We are to quell the human infestation until our apothecaries can develop the new plague.
Our information leads us to believe that the town of Hillsbrad is vulnerable to attack. Your first assignment is aimed at disrupting their food supply and infrastructure.
Travel west. Raid the northernmost farms of Hillsbrad Fields. Make sure that the landowners, Farmer Ray and Farmer Getz, are executed as well.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

17
1950 XP
+100 or +250 reputation with Undercity


== Progress ==
As High Executor of Tarren Mill, under the direct command of Varimathras and the Dark Lady, you are subject to follow my command.
Perhaps you take this war with the Alliance lightly?  If such is the case I should behead you right here and now.  Otherwise, travel west to the town of Hillsbrad and wage battle against the humans, as ordered.
Dismissed.


== Completion ==
At ease, <class>. I see you have returned from battle. My sources tell me you performed well on the Hillsbrad front.


== Notes ==
All the targets can be found on the northern side of the Hillsbrad Fields.
Farmers and Farmhands can be found in the northern fields.
Farmer Ray can usually be found in the large house or in the garden to the east of the big house.
Farmer Getz can usually be found in the barn, in the smaller house, or in the middle field.
