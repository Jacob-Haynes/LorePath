The Gordunni (or the Dire Maul Gordunni clan, the Gordunni Ogres, and the Gordunni clan of ancient Draenor) is an ogre tribe or clan based in central Feralas. They are despoiling the forest and its wildlife. Many have taken residency in the Ruins of Isildien in the High Wilderness as well as the foothills north of Camp Mojache, while the "upper class" is found in Dire Maul. Like most of their kind, Gordunni ogres are remarkably bloodthirsty and are comprised of both melee warriors and spell casters. In the ruins of an ancient night elven city, the Gordunni have established Dire Maul, a stronghold of some potency and power.
