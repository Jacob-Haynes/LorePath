This quest is best done together with the  [20] The Everstill Bridge quest because the gnolls drop the items you need for this.


== Objectives ==
Kill 10 Redridge Brutes and 8 Redridge Mystics, then return to Verner Osgood.

Redridge Brute slain (10)
Redridge Mystic slain (8)


== Description ==
As if the attacking orcs weren't enough! Now I have gnoll brutes and mystics prowling along the ridge north of my stable, taking my horses when they stray...
If you can get rid of those gnolls, my horses and I would be very grateful.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

1,150 XP
9
+75 or +150 reputation with Stormwind


== Progress ==
