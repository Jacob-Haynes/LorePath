Vital Intelligence concludes the Deathknell portion of the Forsaken starting experience, sending players onward to Brill. Tirisfal Glades quests continue with the plethora of quests in Brill, including Zygand's  [8] At War With The Scarlet Crusade and  [6] Patience; and  [6] Discipline in Forever.


== Objectives ==
Deliver the Scarlet Crusade Documents to Executor Zygand in Brill.

 [Scarlet Crusade Documents] (provided)


== Description ==
These will need to be taken to my superior, Executor Zygand, in Brill. With this information, we'll be able to deal a decisive blow to the Scarlet Crusade. Your services have been useful here in Deathknell, but I need someone to deliver this to Zygand, and I believe he'll be able to find you more appropriate work.
Take the road north out of Deathknell. Soon after entering Tirisfal proper, you'll hit a crossroads. Take the eastern fork, and keep going east. You'll pass Cold Hearth Manor along the way.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

75
340 XP
+75 or +150 reputation with Undercity

== Notes ==
On the way out, pick up  [5] A Rogue's Deal from Calvin Montague at the palisade separating Deathknell from the rest of Tirisfal Glades,  [7] Fields of Grief from Deathguard Simmer at the first fork on the road outside, and  [5] Gordo's Task from Gordo, an abomination patrolling the road on the way to Brill.
Executor Zygand is located at  in Brill, just outside the Brill Town Hall.
