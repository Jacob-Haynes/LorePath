Becoming a Parent is the final quest of the Freedom for All Creatures quest chain. The chain starts with  [47] Freedom for All Creatures in Feralas.


== Objectives ==
Speak to Agnar Beastamer in the Hinterlands.


== Description ==
Whoa, it was a lot more ready than I thought...


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

 [Sprite Darter Egg]
+625 XP
+25 reputation with Darnassus
+25 reputation with Wildhammer Clan


== Completion ==
Here, take the egg... better you have it so it bonds to you and looks to you to be its <dadda/momma>.
Congrats, <name>. You're a parent... kinda.
