== Objectives ==
Find the Caravan Chest in the Fallow Sanctuary, obtain the Wizards' Reagents and bring to Watcher Biggs.

 [Wizards' Reagents]


== Description ==
I was stunned during the attack by the Lost Ones, but before my sight went black I spied one of our wagons being dragged to the Fallow Sanctuary, the lost one village to the north.
A caravan chest with valuable reagents was in that wagon, reagents vital to the studies of the wizards of Nethergarde Keep. Find the chest and get those reagents, <name>. Get them and bring them to me.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

1650 XP (or 16 80 at level 70)
+250 reputation with Stormwind


== Progress ==
Do you have the wizard's reagents, <name>?


== Completion ==
Ah, you found them! With these, the wizards in Nethergarde may continue their work. I do not know what it is that they do, but it is linked to the Dark Portal in the Blasted Lands, and is vital to the security of the Alliance.
