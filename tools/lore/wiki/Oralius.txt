Oralius is a dwarf quest giver located at Morgan's Vigil in the Burning Steppes. He appears to be insane due to some sort of war shock and he seems to have an imaginary pirate friend named Captain Winky (there does, however, happen to be a female gnome pirate named Winkey).

 This section concerns content related to Cataclysm.
He also appears in Blackrock Depths.

 This section concerns content related to Warlords of Draenor.
When the Iron Horde retook Blackrock Spire, he traveled to Lunarfall Inn / Frostwall Tavern in order to get help in finding Winky in the spire.
