The Darkspear Islands (also known as the Darkspear Island or Darkspear Isle) were the former home of the Darkspear tribe of jungle trolls, located somewhere within the South Seas or somewhere else in the Great Sea. The islands were volcanically active.
In World of Warcraft: Forever, Darkspear Islands is a battleground similar to Eye of the Storm.
