== Objectives ==
Speak to Captain Aransas in Booty Bay.


== Description ==
Listen, I can't repay you right now, but I'm a cook on Captain Aransas' ship. I'll be sure to put in a good word for you when I get back to Booty Bay. You should speak to the captain when you get the chance. I'm sure she'll be grateful to have me back.


== Rewards ==
You will be able to choose one of these rewards: 

Kurzen Headmaster's Cinch
Jailer's Discarded Chain
Crusty Cuffs
You will also receive:

2850 XP
45


== Completion ==
Ah, ye must be <name>. Mathers tells me ye busted out of Kurzen's compound together. Said the two of ye really let some heads roll. Some proper swashbucklers the pair of ye are!
Ha har! I know it's all a fib, matey. Mathers is a good boy, but the only blade he's fit to wield is a kitchen knife.
All the same, I appreciate ye saving a member of me crew. A good cook is priceless in this line o' work.
