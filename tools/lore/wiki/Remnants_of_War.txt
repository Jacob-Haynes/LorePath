== Objectives ==
Gather 12 Necrotic Crystal Fragments in Tirisfal Glades.

Necrotic Crystal Fragment (12)


== Description ==
It wasn't long ago that the citadel of Naxxramas bore down upon this city, intent on wiping the Forsaken off the map. Both the Forsaken and the Argent Dawn lost much that day to secure the future of the Forsaken.
During the battle, the great necrotic focusing crystal mounted atop Naxxramas was shattered and its fragments were scattered all over this area. I suspect that is what the cultists are here for. Help me recover the fragments and deprive the Scourge of them.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

910 XP
+150 reputation with Argent Dawn


== Progress ==
I'd advise you not come into too much physical contact with those crystals. Even shattered and spent, the lingering magic in these necrotic crystals is highly dangerous.


== Completion ==
Thank you. This should slow the efforts of the Scourge in this area. I suspect the denizens of Tirisfal will be pulling these crystals out of the ground for years, but removing the most conspicuous of them can only help.
