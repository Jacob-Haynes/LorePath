== Objectives ==
Bring 8 Red Burlap Bandanas to Deputy Willem outside the Northshire Abbey.

 [Red Burlap Bandana] (8)


== Description ==
Recently, a new group of thieves has been hanging around Northshire. They call themselves the Defias Brotherhood, and have been seen across the river to the east.
I don't know what they're up to, but I'm sure it's not good! Bring me the bandanas they wear, and I'll reward you with a weapon.


== Rewards ==
You will be able to choose one of these rewards:

 [Militia Dagger]
 [Militia Hammer]
 [Militia Shortsword]
 [Militia Warhammer]
 [Militia Quarterstaff]
You will also receive:

360 XP (or 2 10 at max level)
+250 reputation with Stormwind


== Progress ==
Have you gathered those bandanas for me yet?


== Completion ==
Back with some bandanas, I see.  The Stormwind Army appreciates your help.
