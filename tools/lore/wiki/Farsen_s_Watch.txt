Farsen's Watch begins the attunement quest chain to Hall of Thanes in Forever. Find Earthseer Farsen overlooking Gol'Bolar Quarry to begin.


== Objectives ==
Speak with Earthseer Farsen and ask him to lend you his farsight to observe the Dark Iron threat.

Use Farsen's Farsight


== Description ==
Greetings to ye, friend. My name is Earthseer Farsen and I'm visiting Dun Morogh from Aerie Peak. As I was travelin', I couldn't help but notice that we have, well... a bit o' a Dark Iron Dwarf problem.
If ye have a moment, I can show ye what I mean. I've got a bit o' magic that should do the trick.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

680 XP
+75 reputation with Ironforge
+75 reputation with Gnomeregan Exiles


== Progress ==
Farsight can show ye a great many things, even things ye'd rather not see.


== Completion ==
So ye see now, <class>? Thick as cockroaches they are, and that's just the ones we can see from the sky.
Ye look like a sturdy enough sort. Hows about a bit o' violence?


== Notes ==
Speak with Farsen again:

Farsen puts players in a vehicle, buffing them with Farsen's Far Sight, showing a bunch of Dark Iron Spies and Rockjaw Boneshapers. Click off the buff to exit.
