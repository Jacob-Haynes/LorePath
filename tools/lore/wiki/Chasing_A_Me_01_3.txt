== Objectives ==
Escort A-Me 01 back to Karna Remtravel in Un'Goro Crater.

Protect A-Me 01 until you reach Karna Remtravel


== Description ==
Checking to see if you're prepared to lead A-Me 01 back to Karna Remtravel, you flip the switch to the "Off" position. Glancing around once again to make sure none of the gorillas are coming, you prepare to see what happens when you flip the switch back to the "On" position.
This is your last chance... are you sure this is what you want to do?


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

1 60
6,550 XP


== Progress ==

== Notes ==
Karna Remtravel is at the bottom of Marshal's Refuge. A-Me 01 does fight nearby gorillas but you need to help. It might be easier to clear the path to the mouth of the cave first if there are respawns.
