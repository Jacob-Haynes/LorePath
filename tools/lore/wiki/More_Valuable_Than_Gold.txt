More Valuable Than Gold is the second secret quest in Forever's Alcaz Prison dungeon, only available to players who skip the first boss, The Questioner, and find Mason Connery in the end of the dungeon. Pick up the quest, then return to kill the Questioner for its  [Ornate Shackle Key].
This is part of the secret to obtain the  [Alcaz Operative Insignia]. Read  [Mason's Note] to Find the Loose Floorboard upstairs at  in the Moonbrook inn of Westfall to obtain a  [Document Box]. Don't sell it to vendors for 25, but instead take it to Renzik "The Shiv" in the SI:7 building of Old Town, Stormwind City for Alliance players, or Rix Xizzix in Booty Bay, Stranglethorn Vale and they'll start the next quest,  [53] State Secrets.


== Objectives ==
Use the note provided by Mason Connery to find his hidden treasure.

Find Mason Connery's Treasure
 [Mason's Note] (provided)


== Description ==
I told you that I'd make it worth your while to free me. I don't have any gold for you, but I have something better.
Take this. It will help you find a treasure more valuable than gold. Do what you want with it, but if anyone asks you where you got it, tell them that the person who gave it to you died. 
Tell them he was vaporized by dragonfire... blown out to sea.
