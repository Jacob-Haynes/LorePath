== Objectives ==
Slay the huge gnoll Hogger and bring his Huge Gnoll Claw to Marshal Dughan.

 [Huge Gnoll Claw]


== Description ==
Wanted: Hogger
A huge gnoll, Hogger, is prowling the woods in southwestern Elwynn. He has overpowered all attempts at his capture.
The Stormwind Army has placed a generous bounty on the Gnoll. To earn the reward, bounty hunters should bring proof of Hogger's demise to Marshal Dughan in Goldshire.


== Rewards ==
You will be able to choose one of these rewards:

 [Footman Tunic]
 [Stormwind Guard Leggings]
 [Balanced Fighting Stick]
 [Apprentice Wizard's Gown] 
You will also receive:

875 XP
+100 reputation with Stormwind

== Notes ==
Hogger can be found wandering among the gnoll camps near the southern end of Forest's Edge. As he is a level 11 elite, you may need to group up with another player or two to kill him.
In Forever, the quest name has been changed to WANTED: "Hogger".
