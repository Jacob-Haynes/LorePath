== Objectives ==
Take the Cleverly Encrypted Letter to Prospector Stormpike in Ironforge.

 [Cleverly Encrypted Letter] (provided)


== Description ==
I have a colleague in Ironforge who may be able to help decrypt this letter. Go to the Library in the Hall of Explorers in Ironforge and speak with Prospector Stormpike. Tell him I sent you and show him the letter. If there's anyone north of Blackrock Spire who can read this, it is he.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

1350 XP (or 8 40 at max level)
+75 reputation with Ironforge
