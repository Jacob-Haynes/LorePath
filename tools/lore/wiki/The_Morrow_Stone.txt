This quest concludes the quest chain In Search of Knowledge.


== Objectives ==
Return the Sparkling Stone and the Stave of Equinex to Troyas Moonbreeze in Feathermoon Stronghold. 

 [A Sparkling Stone]
 [Stave of Equinex] (provided)


== Description ==
The moment the artifact is removed from the Monolith, you feel the energy in the Stave of Equinex begin to fade.


== Rewards ==
You will be able to choose one of these rewards:

 [Cairnstone Sliver]
 [Seedtime Hoop]
You will also receive:

4700 XP (or 28 20 at max level)
+250 reputation with Darnassus
