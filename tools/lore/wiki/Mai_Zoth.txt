Mai'Zoth is an ogre mage found in the Mosh'Ogg Ogre Mound in Northern Stranglethorn. Mai'Zoth used an artifact called the  [Mind's Eye] to corrupt Colonel Kurzen. Both Brother Nimetz and Kin'weelay want the Mind's Eye; Nimetz to stop others from suffering the same fate as Kurzen, and Kin'weelay to save Yenniku from the mind-altering elixirs of Zanzil the Outcast.
Mai'Zoth seemingly survived the attempts on his life. Following the war against the Primalists, Rokhan stated that Mai'Zoth should have died long ago, but his magics were still strong. When the Darkspear Chieftain and Kevo ya Siti were looking for Lukou, they found her inside the mound, with Rokhan being sure the ogre mage was holding the loa, so he sent the Darkspear champion to kill Mai'Zoth and free Lukou.
