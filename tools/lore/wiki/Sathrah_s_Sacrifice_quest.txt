Sathrah's Sacrifice is the third quest in the The Temple of the Moon quest chain.


== Objectives ==
Priestess A'moora wants you to place Lady Sathrah's silvery spinnerets at the fountain inside the temple, and then return to her.

Offer the sacrifice at the fountain
 [Sathrah's Sacrifice] (provided)


== Description ==
Now you must finish the task to which you have been appointed. Take the spinnerets and offer Lady Sathrah's sacrifice to Elune; lay the great spider to rest.
Inside this temple, find the central fountain. There is where you may leave the sacrifice. The holy waters will purify and cleanse the corruption that drove Sathrah mad.
When you have completed this, return to me.


== Rewards ==
You will be able to choose one of these rewards:

 [Lace Pants]
 [Cushioned Boots]
You will also receive:

900 XP
+100 or +250 reputation with Darnassus


== Progress ==
Have you placed the sacrifice at the fountain, <name>?


== Completion ==
The loss of Lady Sathrah is distressing, but in this way only may she be reborn with a renewed spirit.
May Elune willingly accept the sacrifice that you have offered her.


== Notes ==
Item used

The Spirit of Sathrah appears in the fountain and walks around.
Spirit of Sathrah is once again at peace.
