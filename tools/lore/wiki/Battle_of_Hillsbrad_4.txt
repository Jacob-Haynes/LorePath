== Objectives ==
Kill Magistrate Burnside and 5 Hillsbrad Councilmen. Destroy the Hillsbrad Proclamation. Steal the Hillsbrad Town Registry. Report back to Darthalia in Tarren Mill afterwards.

Magistrate Burnside slain
Hillsbrad Councilmen slain (5)
Hillsbrad Proclamation destroyed
 [Hillsbrad Town Registry]


== Description ==
The town of Hillsbrad is in disarray and we believe it will soon fall. We must, however, persist in our ruthlessness until the human scum are wiped from the foothills for eternity.
Despite their overwhelming losses, the humans rally around their leadership and keep fighting. We must eliminate their leader, Magistrate Burnside, as well as the town council. Destroy their revered political document as well, the Hillsbrad Proclamation. And while you're at it, steal the town registry.

== Notes ==
There's a large building which is the Town Hall. Burnside, the Proclamation, and the Registry are all inside. Councilmen are both outside and inside the hall.
Burnside and the councilmen are all mages so you have to deal with ranged attacks. They will assist each other so you might have to take 2-3 on at once.
The Proclamation is in front of Burnside.
The Registry is in the corner by the stairs leading up to Burnside's platform. It looks like a book.
