Orendil's Cure will hopefully save a young child's life.


== Objectives ==
Bring Orendil's Cure to Pelturas Whitemoon in Astranaar.  

 [Orendil's Cure] (provided)


== Description ==
<Name>, I have created a cure that I believe will help the afflicted child. Take it to Astranaar and give it to the child's parent, Pelturas Whitemoon.
To reach Astranaar, follow the road south, then east.   


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

25


== Progression ==
I must apologize, for I have no time to speak.  My child, Relara, is deathly ill.


== Completion ==
This is from Orendil?
His skill with herbs and healing is great.  This cure gives me hope, when before I had little...


== Notes ==
Follow the main road south, then east to Astranaar.

Upon completion
Pelturas kneels by the bed.
Pelturas Whitemoon says: Here you are, Relara. Take this...
Relara Whitemoon drinks the cure brought by <name>.
Pelturas drops to his knees and cries.
Relara Whitemoon says: Uh... Father...? Are you there? I'm hungry... Can I have some stew?
Pelturas Whitemoon says: What?
Pelturas Whitemoon says: You are awake?? Your cure must have worked! Thank you, <name>.
Pelturas bows toward the adventurer.
