The Shade of the Archmage is the last boss in the City of Dalaran instance, found at the Purple Parlor atop the Violet Citadel.


== Abilities ==
 Arcane Bolt — Inflicts 15 to 19 Arcane damage to an enemy.
 Arcane Explosion — Sends out a blast wave of magic, inflicting 9 to 11 Arcane damage to nearby enemies.
 Bounding Mana — Launches a mana infused orb toward a random player that travels out and returns to its origin. Players hit are inflicted with 285 to 315 Arcane damage.
 Evocation — While channeling this spell, your mana regenerates at an accelerated rate.
 Mass Polymorph — Transforms nearby enemies into an animal, forcing it to wander around for up to 8 sec. While wandering, they cannot attack or cast spells.
 Slow — Increases the time between nearby enemies' attacks by 30% and slows their movement and casting speed by 70% for 3 sec.
