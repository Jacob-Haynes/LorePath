This quest is part of quest chain The Story of Morgan Ladimore.


== Objectives ==
Kill Mor'Ladim, then return his skull to Commander Althea Ebonlocke in Darkshire.

 [Mor'Ladim's Skull]
Suggested Players [3]


== Description ==
From what my scouts tell me, Mor'Ladim wanders throughout Duskwood, following a strange and meandering path through the cemetery. We buried him out past the house on the hill, you know where that is?
There isn't anything I can do to help you, but I wish you good luck.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

18
1500 XP (165 XP at grey level)
+150 reputation with Stormwind

== Notes ==
Return to north of Forlorn Rowe (take the flight path back to Raven Hill!), where at  is A Weathered Grave.
Mor'Ladim wanders around the cemetery, but does eventually stop at the grave marker. A level 25 elite skeletal warrior, Mor'Ladim has 4,194 health and the following abilities:

Holy Shock 30 yd range — Blasts the target with Holy energy, causing Holy damage to an enemy Instant.
Enraging Memories — Drives the caster into a maddened frenzy for 8 sec. Increases the caster's attack speed by 50%, but slows its movement speed by 25% and reduces the Physical damage it deals by 10. (25 sec cooldown)
The game suggests a group, which is strongly advised. Take him out, then fly back to Darkshire.
