== Objectives ==
Travel to Lakeshire and deliver Helendis Riverhorn's Letter to Magistrate Solomon. 

 [Helendis Riverhorn's Letter] (provided)


== Description ==
We had always assumed that the Blackrock orcs were a product
of their savage upbringing. What we have found here, in their homeland, tells
us another story.
Indeed, it is bloodlust and thirst for conflict that empowers them, but the
black dragonflight are the beings whom control the orcs of Blackrock. We suspect that it is the black dragonflight that fuels them with rage and hostility.
Take this letter to Magistrate Solomon. He will know what must be done.
<Helendis hands you a sealed letter.>


== Progress ==
Greetings, <name>. Have we met before? You look vaguely familiar. 


== Completion ==
<Magistrate Solomon opens the sealed letter from Helendis and begins reading.>


== Notes ==
You may have encountered Magistrate Solomon before in the Defense of Lakeshire quest chain.
