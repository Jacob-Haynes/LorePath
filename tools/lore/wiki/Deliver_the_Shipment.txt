== Description ==
While I fix this wagon, <name>, I have one last task for you.
I have packed the most vital supplies saved from the ogre attacks. Take them to Nethergarde Keep. The keep is nestled in the northern hills of the Blasted Lands, keeping watch over the Dark Portal.
To reach Nethergarde, follow the road east. When it branches head south into the Blasted Lands, then turn left at the next fork. Follow the road, <name>, and be wary.
