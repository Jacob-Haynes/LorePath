Durgen Dirgehammer is the fourth and last boss in the Hall of Thanes instance, found at the Reliquary of Kings. He is accompanied by two Lesser Stone Golems.


== Abilities ==
 Heroic Strike — A strong attack that increases melee damage by 11 and causes a high amount of threat.
 Intimidating Shout — Shouts at an enemy, paralyzing it with terror for 8 sec. and causing all other nearby enemies to flee in fear.
 Rend — Inflicts Physical damage to an enemy every 3 sec. for 15 sec.
