== Objectives ==
Resurrect, then look for the gravestone that Gaeriyan told you to find.

 [Linken's Tempered Sword] (provided)


== Description ==
If you return to the graveyard, <name>, you will find a gravestone there... Not just any gravestone; this one will stand out from the others. Secrets lie within it.
Push on the stone, and you will learn of these secrets...


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

3650 XP


== Progress ==
This must be the gravestone that Gaeriyan mentioned...


== Completion ==

You push on the stone firmly, and it slides away, revealing a small depression underneath it.
Placing Linken's Sword into the depression, you realize that it fits perfectly...


== Notes ==
On completion, a green pillar of light briefly appears above the gravestone.
