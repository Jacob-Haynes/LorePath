Wheeler's Grange is a farmstead near Farholde Keep in the Riverglades in World of Warcraft: Forever. Benjy Wheeler can be found outside the farmhouse, while hostile harvest reapers occupy the fields.


== Inhabitants ==
Benjy Wheeler
Harvest reapers


== Speculation ==

The farmstead may belong to Benjy Wheeler, as it shares his surname and he stands outside the farmhouse.
