== Objectives ==
Defeat Shadra at the altar of Shadra'Alor, then bring her venom to Apothecary Lydon in Tarren Mill.

 [Shadra's Venom]
 [Gadrin's Parchment] (provided)


== Description ==
Take his parchment, and read it at the altar of Shadra'Alor, deep within the witherbark realm in the Hinterlands. This will summon Shadra the spider god.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

8,450 XP
+200 reputation with Undercity
