The Relic Guardian is the third and last boss in the Excavation Site: Wetlands instance, found at the Site of the Guardian.


== Abilities ==
 Arcing Smash — Lashes out in a vicious arc, inflicting damage to enemies in a cone in front of the caster.
 Knock Away — Inflicts normal damage plus 30 to an enemy and knocks it back.
 Stasis
 Thunderclap — Inflicts 24 to 26 Nature damage to nearby enemies, increasing the time between their attacks by 33% and slowing their movement by 40% for 10 sec.
 War Stomp — Inflicts normal damage plus 23 to nearby enemies, knocking them back and stunning them for 5 sec.
