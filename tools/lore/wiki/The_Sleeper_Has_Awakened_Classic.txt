== Objectives ==
Escort Kerlonian Evershade to Liladris Moonriver at Maestra's Post in Ashenvale.

Escort Kerlonian Evershade to Maestra's Post
 [Horn of Awakening]


== Description ==
I was on my way to Maestra's Post to meet Liladris Moonriver, but I decided to stop here and take a short nap. Ever since I awoke from the emerald dream, I have been so sleepy...
<yawn>
Would you mind showing me the way? I understand that Maestra's Post is south of here, along the road in Ashenvale. It can be dangerous, so I hope you have some friends that will join us.
Lastly, I may fall asleep so if you ever need to wake me, please use my horn. You'll find it in the chest here.

== Notes ==
On accept:

Kerlonian Evershade says: Liladris has been waiting for me at Maestra's Post, so we should make haste, <name>.
Kerlonian will follow the player who started the quest. Every now and then he stops to lay down, with an emote:

Kerlonian Evershade begins to drift off...
Kerlonian Evershade looks very sleepy...
Kerlonian Evershade suddenly falls asleep.
...and a /say line:

Kerlonian Evershade says: Oh, I am so tired...
Kerlonian Evershade says: This looks like the perfect place for a nap...
Kerlonian Evershade says: Yaaaaawwwwwnnnn...
Kerlonian Evershade says: You don't mind if I stop here for a moment, do you?
Use the  [Horn of Awakening] to rouse him:

Kerlonian Evershade wakes up!
In the Twilight Vale:

Kerlonian Evershade says: Be on the alert! The Blackwood furbolgs are numerous in this area...
A Blackwood Shaman and two Blackwood Ursa attack.
In Ashenvale:

Kerlonian Evershade says: It's quiet... Too quiet...
A Dark Strand Adept, Dark Strand Cultist, and Dark Strand Excavator attack.
At Maestra's Post:

Kerlonian Evershade says: Oh, I can see Liladris from here... Tell her I'm here, won't you?
Kerlonian walks off to the side of the building and disappears.
Throughout the quest, Kerlonian uses Druid of the Claw voice lines from Warcraft III. These include the following:

On accept: "The sleeper has awakened."
When awakened: "Anu'dora," "I'd rather be hibernating," "I'm awake... I'm awake," "My path is clear," and "Our time is short."
In combat: "Bear arms!" and "I'll make short work of them!"
Because Kerlonian follows the player rather than a preset path, it's possible to avoid the scripted encounters by staying away from the road.
The Horn of Awakening has a 25 minute time limit before it disappears. This can be an issue if the quest is failed; talking to Kerlonian to start the escort again will not reset the horn's timer, which may unexpectedly doom a second attempt. The timer can be reset by destroying the horn and picking up a new one from the chest.
