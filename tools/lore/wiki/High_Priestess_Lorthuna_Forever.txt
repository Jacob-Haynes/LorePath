High Priestess Lorthuna is a Skyborne and the leader of the Al'Aketh, a cult worshiping Al'Akir the Windlord. On orders of Talaanis Shadowsong, Skyborne adventurers spied on Lorthuna. After the Al'Aketh brought down the wards keeping Al'Akir and his Conclave of Wind from noticing Zephras Isle, Lorthuna lit a beacon to call their attention, and Rohash appeared. She demanded to be presented to Al'Akir so he could know who it was that told him about the Skyborne, and in response Rohash knocked her aside, sending her flying into the endless sky of Skywall.


== Trivia ==
The reason for the discrepancy between Lorthuna's depiction as a Skyborne in Forever and as a Sin'dorei in Retail is unknown.
