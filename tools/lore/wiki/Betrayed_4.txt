In the final step of Betrayed, you need simply deliver the  [Head of Magus Rimtori] to Belgrom Rockmaul in Orgrimmar. This is the last quest of the chain.


== Objectives ==
Speak to Belgrom Rockmaul in Orgrimmar.

 [Head of Magus Rimtori] (provided)


== Description ==
Return to Belgrom in Orgrimmar. He will want to see her head for himself! We'll return there shortly.
Ah, this is indeed a good day. Thank you for your help, <name>.


== Rewards ==
You will be able to choose one of these rewards:

 [Pyrestone Orb]
 [Belgrom's Hammer]
You will also receive:

7,300 XP / 10,215 XP (Season of Mastery)
+150 reputation with Orgrimmar


== Progress ==
