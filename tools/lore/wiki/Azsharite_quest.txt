This is part of the main Blasted Lands quest chain, although it takes place in Azshara.


== Objectives ==
Recover twenty Crystallized Azsharite from southern Azshara. You may use the Felhound to assist you in 'sniffing' out the location of cleverly hidden deposits.
Return to Loramus when the task is complete.

 [Crystallized Azsharite] (20)
 [Felhound Tracker Kit] (provided)


== Description ==
The ancient cliff giants of the southern region of Azshara consume the rock and buildings of the land for sustenance. The excrement from their consumption is a highly malleable and extremely strong crystal known as Azsharite.
The formations litter the southern arc, but few are ever recovered due to the extremely violent nature of the giants.
You are to venture south and recover enough of the Azsharite for the creation of the weapon's body. My felhounds will aid in the recovery of the crystal.


== Progress ==
Evil does not sleep, fool.  You will have time for rest when you are finally dead. Now bring me those crystals.


== Completion ==
Your tasks in Azshara are complete, mortal.  I must now forge the necessary components for the next step in your adventure.


== Notes ==
You're going to be collecting the big blue crystals on the southern "claw" in Azshara.  When you accept the quest, you'll get a Felhound Tracker Kit, which contains a  [Fel Tracker Owner's Manual] and a  [Fel Orb].  Open the kit quickly, because it has a 10 minute limit.  There's a several-hour timer (and 5 uses) on the orb and no timer on the manual. I found the best place for this was a little stretch around 61,84. More here than most other places.
Read the manual (it's quite amusing as well as informative)!
Most of the Azsharite formations look like big blue crystals, but some of them have a humanoid shape. These are actually digested remains of previous travellers eaten by the cliff giants, and Crystallized Notes can be found inside them as well as azsharite.
If you /roar at your Tracker and it growls back without going anywhere, then there's no Azsharite in its range.
