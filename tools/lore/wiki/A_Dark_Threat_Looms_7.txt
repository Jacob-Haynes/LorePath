== Objectives ==
The Disarming Mixture seemed to take effect. Return to Chief Engineer Hinderweir to report the good news.


== Description ==
The keg fizzles slightly.


== Rewards ==
You will be able to choose one of these rewards:

 [Dwarven Tree Chopper]
 [Thornblade]
You will also receive:

1950 XP (or 12 at max level)
+350 reputation with Ironforge
