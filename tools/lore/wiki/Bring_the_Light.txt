== Objectives ==
Archbishop Bendictus[sic] wants you to slay Amnennar the Coldbringer in Razorfen Downs.

Amnennar the Coldbringer slain


== Description ==
The Scourge is a persistent threat to the Alliance, a fact I am sure you know quite well. Recently, we have come across some very peculiar findings, <name>.
In the Barrens lies a horrid tangle of briars called Razorfen Downs. While once it was the home of simple quilboar, now it has become apparent that a pact was made with the Scourge, creating creatures far worse... They are now ruled by the lich, Amnennar the Coldbringer.
Align yourself with the Light, <name>; put an end to this evil union.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

4,300 or 5,550 XP
+150 or +500 reputation with Stormwind
 [Vanquisher's Sword]
 [Amberglow Talisman]


== Progress ==
Amnennar must be eradicated. Go, swiftly.


== Completion ==
Thank you, <name>. The Scourge will now think twice before attempting to bolster its ranks again.
May you be blessed by the Light.
