== Objectives ==
Help Walton survive the encounter with Terry Longdrink and the Kul Tiras marines.

Assist Walton


== Description ==
Yes, I used to be a member of the Kul Tiras navy. After Lord Admiral took Theramore and started his campaign in Durotar, I had... "objections" to his methods. Particularly the methods involving executing civilians.
There's more to the story but... wait... what's that?
There's a group of men coming up the ridge!
