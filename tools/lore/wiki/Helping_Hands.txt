== Objectives ==
Go with Hurley to save Curly.


== Description ==
Well, I guess that explains why Curly didn't come back for lunch break. Looks like I have to bail him out again -- typical. The way he makes me worry, I swear, I'll be gray in a year.
Come on, we'll have to save him ourselves, and it sounds like we don't have much time.


== Rewards ==
You will be able to choose one of these rewards: 

Curly's Chopper
Hurley's Felling Axe
You will also receive:

3000 XP
+100 reputation with Brotherhood of the Horse


== Completion ==
That damn kid...
Even if he is a knucklehead, Curly's a good worker. Hopefully he heals up fine and learns some sense. Things could've gone a lot worse if you hadn't been there. Thanks for looking out for him.
