Kum'isha the Collector is a Lost One located near the Rise of the Defiler in the Blasted Lands. He believes that greed is the one responsible for the evils in the world and that all races smell of it.
He sought out draenethyst crystals, hoping to use them to return home to Outland. With the help of adventurers Kum'isha was able to open a portal to his old home, which he and several other Lost Ones stepped through.
Despite his earlier success in reaching Outland, Kum'isha was back in the Blasted Lands looking for more draenethyst after the Cataclysm.
He was not present in the Blasted Lands during the invasion of the Iron Horde.
During the events of the war against the Primalists on the Dragon Isles, Kum'isha attended the Tishamaat in the Exodar. He could be seen speaking with Iorioa and Hamanar. He expressed that his desires had always been about going back to his old life that no longer existed, but he himself still exists. After he traveled to Shattrath City, he vowed to make a new life for himself.
