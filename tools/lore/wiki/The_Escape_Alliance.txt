== Objectives ==
Take the Invisibility Liquor to Maybell Maclure.

 [Invisibility Liquor] (provided)


== Description ==
Take this invisibility liquor to young Maybell.  It should last long enough for her to visit Tommy Joe.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

630 XP (or 3 90 at lvl 90)
+250 reputation with Stormwind
5x  [Minor Healing Potion]


== Progress ==
Did you deliver my letter to Tommy Joe?   What did he say??


== Completion ==
Oh...my!  I feel guilty deceiving my family, but my feelings for Tommy Joe are too strong to ignore.
Thank you, <name>.  I'll drink this liquor as soon as I have the chance and sneak away to my love.
And for you... please take this.


== Notes ==
The story drops here, and for many years in the game that was that.  However in World of Warcraft: Warlords of Draenor, the player builds a Garrison, and if they choose to build the Barn, they'll find Tommy Joe Stonefield and Maybell Maclure-Stonefield (and family) working in it.

On completion
Maybell Maclure says: Here goes nothing...
Maybell drinks the liquor and vanishes.
