== Objectives ==
Hemet Nesingwary wants you to bring him the head of King Bangalash, the great white tiger.

 [Head of Bangalash]


== Description ==
<Name>, you are a hunter worthy of our company. From the battlefields of Lordaeron to the deepest jungle, I have rarely witnessed such prowess with weaponry as you have demonstrated.
You have killed the ferocious Sin'Dall, the elusive Bhag'thera, and the treacherous Tethis. But the true prize is the head of King Bangalash. That white tiger is the reason I am here. I've had my sights on him for quite some time now. Kill King Bangalash and your hunting prowess is proven to be second to none.


== Rewards ==
You will be able to choose one of these rewards:

 [Master Hunter's Bow]
 [Master Hunter's Rifle]

60
5,350 XP


== Progress ==

== Completion ==
Bloody well done!  Right-o!  Congratulations, <name>, for succeeding where others have failed.
I am honored to count you as a comrade of our great hunting party here.  No one will ever doubt your expertise in hunting and tracking!
Cheers to you, I say!  Cheers!


== Notes and trivia ==
Big Game Hunter is a common quest card in the Trading Card Game. Its flavor text reads: "Killing for survival is savage. Killing for sport is civilized."
