The Tarnished continues the Forever-exclusive storyline for Forsaken paladins at Bandarion Keep in Tirisfal Glades alongside    [11] Making Repairs.


== Objectives ==
Slay 8 Tarnished Drudges, 6 Tarnished Zealots, and collect the head of Commander Rudolph Gelhardt in Tirisfal Glades.

Rudolph Gelhardt's Head 
Tarnished Drudge slain (8)
Tarnished Zealot slain (6)


== Description ==
Not all risen paladins have acclimated well to our new existence, as you may have seen yourself already. Some have carried forward their prejudices from life, and continue to visit violence upon their fellow Forsaken.
As it stands we are mistrusted by our people who remember well the persecution inflicted by the living in the name of the light, and these attacks do not help. A number of these tarnished have taken up residence at Shadowvale to the southwest. End them and bring me their leader's head.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

5
910 XP
+50 reputation with Undercity


== Progress ==
The Tarnished threaten what little goodwill we have with the other Forsaken. As much as it pains me to cross blades with our former brothers and sisters, they must be stopped if our fledgling order is to survive.


== Completion ==
Excellent work, <name>. Every time one of these Tarnished strike out and kill one of the Forsaken, it becomes that much easier for our detractors to stoke mistrust towards us paladins. Eliminating them will help further our cause and solidify our place here in Tirisfal.
I suspect it may take a long time for us to be fully embraced by the Forsaken and the horde. It could take years... or maybe even decades, but the best we can do is keep trying to prove ourselves.


== Notes ==
Pick up    [11] Making Repairs before heading out. This is also a good time to complete  [10] That Shadowvale Green Elixir from Brill. Head southwest of the lake to make it to Shadowvale.
Tarnished Zealots are former Scarlet Crusade, so if players have not yet finished  [7] Proof of Demise, they will drop their  [Scarlet Insignia Ring]. Rudolph Gelhardt is upstairs in the farmhouse.
The quest items for the other quests are below ground in the root cellar. The stairs down to it are at  in the ruined building on the south side of the village.
