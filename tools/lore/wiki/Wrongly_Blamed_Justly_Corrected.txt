== Objectives ==
Slay Alliance soldiers encroaching into the Barrens at the border to Dustwallow Marsh, then return to Grunt Logmar at Camp Taurajo.

Encroaching Soldier slain (2)
Outraged Pillager slain


== Description ==
Traveler, have you plans to head toward Dustwallow Marsh? I have heard mutterings that a few human soldiers have taken a stand at its border and are toppling the landmarks left behind by our tauren pathfinders.
Rumor has it that a favored tavern has burned to the ground, and in typical Alliance fashion, they immediately cast their blame onto the Horde! I cannot know whether there is any truth to their claim, but their incursion onto OUR land must not stand.
Kill first, investigate later!


== Rewards ==
You will be able to choose one of these rewards:

 [Encroacher's Claim]
 [Pathfinder's Clearway]
 [Traveler's Shawl]
You will also receive:

2,300 XP


== Completion ==
So the rumors were true! I expected as much, tensions have been rising along the eastern coast for some time. I knew it would only be a matter of time before conflict crept into our territory.
However, I am skeptical that we played a role in the tavern's destruction. Too many Horde civilians rely on that route for trade and travel. Perhaps you could investigate further if you find the time.
In any case, today's deed is a victory for the Horde, and a stern warning to the Alliance!
