Delivery to the Gnomes is a neutral quest in the contested zone Thousand Needles. It is the final quest in a delivery quest chain in which players are sent to Ratchet to pick up some parts for the gnomes of Mirage Raceway.


== Objectives ==
Bring the  [Delicate Car Parts] to Fizzle Brassbolts in Shimmering Flats.

 [Delicate Car Parts] (provided)


== Description ==
Now that I have what I need, give these parts to Fizzle Brassbolts. He's been waiting a long time for them -- I'm sure he won't miss the little piece I removed from his order...
And I wouldn't bother mentioning it to him. It was small and worthless.
Trust me.


== Reward ==
You will receive:
10


== Completion ==
There they are! My hydro ratchets and repeater bolts! These will do just fine. Thank you!
