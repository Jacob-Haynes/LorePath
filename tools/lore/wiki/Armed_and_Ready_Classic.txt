This quest is part of the Morbent Fel quest chain.


== Objectives ==
Bring Morbent's Bane to Sven in Duskwood.

 [Morbent's Bane] (provided)


== Description ==
Here is the weapon against the fiend of which you spoke. You're all ready, <name>. Best of luck to you, and I look forward to your tale of glory!


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

600 XP
+25 reputation with Stormwind


== Progress ==
Hello, <name>.  You haven't been gone that long, but I see a fire in your eyes I did not see before.


== Completion ==
Ah, this will be proof against the fiend Morbent Fel!  Well done!
You are ready.  Morbent Fel will fall.


== Notes ==
A usual, if you lose Morbent's Bane for some reason, you can drop this quest and accept it again, to get it back.
You can get to Sven faster from Sentinel Hill in Westfall than from Darkshire in Duskwood.
