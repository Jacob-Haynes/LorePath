== Objectives ==
Kill 10 Kul Tiras Sailors, 8 Kul Tiras Marines and Lieutenant Benedict and return to Gar'Thok in Razor Hill.

Kul Tiras Sailor slain (10)
Kul Tiras Marine slain (8)
Lieutenant Benedict slain


== Description ==
Led by Admiral Proudmoore, the humans of Kul Tiras encroached on Durotar, violating the Warchief's pact made with Jaina Proudmoore in order to defeat Archimonde years ago.
The human aggression was repelled and Tiragarde Keep fell. But recently, the Admiral's reserves, led by Lieutenant Benedict, have retaken the keep and once again pose a threat to our homeland. These humans show no respect for diplomacy.
Prove your honor and travel south to Tiragarde Keep to eliminate the human invaders.

== Completion ==
Word of your bravery travels fast, <class>. Tales of your victory at Tiragarde Keep will be heralded in Orgrimmar.


== Notes ==
The Kul Tiras Sailors and Kul Tiras Marines are outside and inside Tiragarde Keep southwest of Razor Hill and northeast of Sen'jin Village.
Lieutenant Benedict is inside the keep on the second floor. He usually has 3 Kul Tiras Marines inside the room with him, so you may need a small group to defeat him.
Completion of this quest will allow one to receive the quest.
It is a good idea to have one inventory spot open to retrieve  [Benedict's Key] off Lieutenant Benedict's corpse. This key in turn will open a chest on the third floor of Tiragarde keep, which contains an  [Aged Envelope], which allows one to begin  [7] The Admiral's Orders quest.
