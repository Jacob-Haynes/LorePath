Lord Gregor Lescovar was a human located in the Stormwind Library. He was a high ranking member of the House of Nobles. He secretly worked with the Defias Brotherhood and could be exposed by players on a quest chain. He was also under suspicion of being a contact for the Twilight's Hammer. He had an employee known only as Marzon. Both were killed, thanks to the effort of Tyrion, his spybot and the adventurer, on the orders of Elling Trias.
