== Objectives ==
Find a clue in the Twilight's Hammer camp at the Master's Glaive.


== Description ==
The Twilight's Hammer's purpose at the Master's Glaive is not known. But it must be dark.
Search through their camp at the Glaive... Search for some thing that may teach us their intentions.
For we must learn their intentions before a plan is made.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

9
1250 XP (or 7 80 at level 70)
250 reputation with Darnassus


== Completion ==
This book is hand printed in an ancient language, and illustrated by a master artist.


== Notes ==
You need to go to the northern tome to finish the quest, the southern one won't do it, and has a chance of knocking you down.
