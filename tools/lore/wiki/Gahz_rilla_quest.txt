Gahz'rilla is a neutral quest for the dungeon Zul'Farrak.  This was one of the more complex quests for Zul'Farrak because it used to require obtaining a mallet (see below for details) to summon him, but it was often considered worth it for the speed-increasing item.
Note that  [46D] The Brassbolts Brothers is not required for this quest, but is a lead-in for Alliance players.


== Objectives ==
Bring Gahz'rilla's Electrified Scale to Wizzle Brassbolts in the Shimmering Flats.

 [Gahz'rilla's Electrified Scale]


== Description ==
Deep in Zul'Farrak, the sand troll city in Tanaris, there is a sacred pool. From that pool the trolls summon a huge beast! Gahz'rilla! He's so fierce that even his scales crackle with energy. It's that energy I want to harness for my car!
Bring me the electrified scale of Gahz'rilla!
But the summoning of Gahz'rilla is a well-kept secret of the trolls. To face him, you must first wrest the secret from them.
