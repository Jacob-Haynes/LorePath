== Objectives ==
Find Magma Lord Bokk in the Burning Steppes, obtain his Magma Core and bring it to Mux Manascrambler in Gadgetzan.

 [Magma Core]


== Description ==
Next we shall need to find a stable and portable source of energy to keep the ectoplasm active. I've heard rumors of a mighty elemental called Magma Lord Bokk, whose heart is a powerful Magma Core - an endless supply of heat!
It's said he can be found in the Burning Steppes, south of Blackrock Mountain. Bring the Magma Core to me and we shall have enough juice to power the extra-dimensional ghost revealer!


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

90
9550 XP (or a 90 compensation at level 80)


== Progress ==
Have you found the Magma Lord, <name>?


== Completion ==
You've done it!  This will certainly provide enough energy to power up the extra-dimensional ghost revealer!


== Notes ==
Magma Lord Bokk can be found west of the Pillar of Ash and south of Blackrock Mountain. He is a non-elite level 60.
