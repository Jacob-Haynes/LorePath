This is the final quest in the Raene's Cleansing quest chain.
It used to be that this part of the chain was often declined by alliance players wishing to keep their furbolg form, as the quest required you to give up  [Dartol's Rod of Transformation], which is often considered the main reason for doing the chain.  However, in the most recent patches, players keep the rod after the quest is over.


== Objectives ==
Bring Ran Bloodtooth's Skull to Raene Wolfrunner in Astranaar.

 [Ran Bloodtooth's Skull] (provided)


== Description ==
Return to your own tribe and let them know of your victory here.
Show them your trophy, and bask in the glory...


== Rewards ==
You will be able to choose one of these rewards: 

 [Glacial Stone]
 [Gutterblade]
You will also receive:

3050 XP
+350 reputation with Darnassus
 [Ring of Pure Silver]
 [Replica Dartol's Rod of Transformation]
