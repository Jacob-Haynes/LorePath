Zamael Lunthistle is a night elf ghost quest giver located in a cage on a Twilight's Hammer-controlled hill in the northern part of Firewatch Ridge in Searing Gorge. 
When he was young, Zamael entered the Twilight's Hammer cult. After many years Elune gave him a vision and he recognized the wrong he did. He then started to craft his  [Prayer to Elune], a golden tablet containing his shame. After the Twilight's Hammer members found it, they executed him for blasphemy and his spirit is now unable to fade from this world.
