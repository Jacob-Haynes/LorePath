The Valley of Bones is a crag on the southern edge of Desolace, infested by skeletons and centaur necromancers and haunted by ghosts of the Magram clan. Collapsed against the cliff walls are a pair of massive skeletons of unknown origin called the Dead Goliaths.
In Forever this area is considered part of Shen'dralas instead of Desolace.
