This quest starts the A Sign of Hope quest chain.


== Objectives ==
Find Prospector Ryedol and let him know Hammertoe Grez is alive.


== Description ==
<There is text roughly scribbled in chalk on the map.>
"Ryedol,
They're taking me to Uldaman. Send help.
-Hammertoe"


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

1350 XP (or 8 40 at level 70)
+75 reputation with Ironforge


== Completion ==
Hammertoe's alive?! This is amazin', <name>!
