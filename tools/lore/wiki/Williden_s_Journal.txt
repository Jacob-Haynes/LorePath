== Objective ==
Return the journal to Williden Marshal somewhere in Un'Goro Crater.

 [A Mangled Journal] (provided)


== Description ==
You have uncovered a mangled journal. Parts of it remain legible, with a Williden Marshal claiming ownership over the contents therein. If the journal is accurate, then Williden may be found somewhere in the northern part of the Crater; according to the journal, he and his companions sought higher ground from the savage beasts of the Crater once their base camps were overrun.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

5100 XP
