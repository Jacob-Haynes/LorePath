The Battle of Darrowshire was a conflict between a united force of humans, and a large army of Undead Scourge, over the fate of the Lordaeronian village of Darrowshire during the Third War.
When the Scourging of Lordaeron began, the village of Darrowshire was cut off from the remainder of the forces of the Alliance of Lordaeron. As it became further threatened by the undead, a unified defense force was established to protect the settlement and its inhabitants, with a combination of Lordaeron troops, paladins of the Silver Hand, and a thrown-together group of militia led by Captain Joseph Redpath.
Although Darrowshire's defenders were successful in repelling major undead attacks, even slaying ghoul lord Horgus, the tide of the battle turned after Captain Redpath's spirit was twisted and corrupted by the death knight Marduk the Black. Once he spread his taint to the other humans, who fell under the sway of the Scourge, the defenders turned on Darrowshire and promptly slaughtered all of its residents.
In the aftermath, the remaining undead and the corrupted defenders left the ruined village behind and pressed on to continue to ravage the once-mighty kingdom, adding to the Scourge's assault on the living.
