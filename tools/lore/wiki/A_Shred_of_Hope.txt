This is part of the Great Masquerade quest chain.


== Objectives ==
Return Marshal Windsor's Lost Information.
Marshal Windsor believes that the information is being held by Golem Lord Argelmach and General Angerforge.

 [Marshal Windsor's Lost Information] (from General Angerforge)
 [Marshal Windsor's Lost Information] (from Golem Lord Argelmach)


== Description ==
<Marshal Windsor shakes the note excitedly.>
Do you realize what this means, <name>?
Perhaps all is not lost! From what I can ascertain, the information is being held by two Dark Iron dwarves: General Angerforge and the golem lord, Argelmach. I have a feeling that they won't just hand over the information
if we ask nicely.
I hope you are ready to battle.
Venture forth and find these two villains! Destroy them and return my lost information. 


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

90
13500 XP (or 37 50 at max level)
+500 reputation with Stormwind


== Progress ==
Soon this grand subterfuge will be over.


== Completion ==
<Marshal Windsor snatches the two tablets from your hands.>
We need to get out of here!


== Notes ==
There are two identically named items, one from General Angerforge, one from Golem Lord Argelmach.  You need them both.
