Zul'Gurub (/ˈzuːlɡʊˌɹuːb/ ZOOL goo-ROOB) is a temple city inhabited by jungle trolls located in northeastern Stranglethorn Vale. Thousands of years ago it was the capital of the Gurubashi Empire and all jungle troll tribes affiliated with them. However, now it only serves as the capital of the Gurubashi tribe.
It is an extremely dangerous place where the influence of Hakkar still persists, the trolls there are brutal, violent and fast. Many people venture there in search of treasures and artifacts. But few come out of it, if not at all.
