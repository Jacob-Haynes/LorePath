Sara Balloo's Plea is part of the A King's Tribute quest chain.


== Objectives ==
Take Sara Balloo's note to King Magni Bronzebeard of Ironforge.

 [Sara Balloo's Plea] (provided)


== Description ==
<Name>, I have but a small request to ask of you.
Take this note I have prepared to our great monarch of Ironforge, King Magni Bronzebeard. 'Tis a small task to ask of one such as yourself, someone who has shown great valor to the cause of the Alliance thus far.
For me to go before the King in my grief-ridden state would not serve the purpose of my cause. Surely you understand.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

1200 XP (or 7 20 at max level)
+75 reputation with Ironforge


== Progress ==
So you are <name> the <class>? News of your contributions to the Alliance has traveled quite far, you know?
On what business do you come before me today?


== Completion ==
It will take but a moment for me to peruse this letter...


== Notes ==
On completion
King Magni Bronzebeard carefully studies Sara Balloo's note and lets out a long, solemn sigh.
