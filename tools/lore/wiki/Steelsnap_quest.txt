Steelsnap is one of many monster-hunting quests given by Melor Stonehoof in Thunder Bluff. In this quest, players are sent to Thousand Needles to kill the ferocious hyena, Steelsnap.


== Objectives ==
Bring Steelsnap's Rib to Melor Stonehoof in Thunder Bluff.

 [Steelsnap's Rib]


== Description ==
At times we hunt for food. At times we hunt for honor. And at times we hunt to earn the Earthmother's teachings.
But the hunt I now set upon you is none of these things. It is to slay a creature outside the Earthmother's blessing.
The hyena Steelsnap roams the Thousand Needles, south of the Barrens. He is a tyrant, attracting other hyenas to him and spreading fear and bloodshed among more peaceful beasts.
Find Steelsnap, <name>. Find him, and defeat him. 


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

2,450 XP
+100 or +250 reputation with Thunder Bluff


== Progress ==
Have you defeated the foul Steelsnap, <name>?


== Completion ==
In defeating the creature Steelsnap, you have cleansed the Thousand Needles of a horrible taint.
And that, <name>, is your lesson this day.


== Notes ==
How to get this quest: Melor Stonehoof can be found on Hunter Rise in Thunder Bluff, coordinates (61,80). Speak with him to receive this quest. Completion of  [30] Melor Sends Word is NOT necessary to receive this quest.
Steelsnap wanders south of Camp E'thok in Thousand Needles (the north-western area), along with two level 29 hyenas. Because of these two additional mobs, this can be a difficult fight. Bringing a friend may be helpful.
After defeating Steelsnap and looting his rib, return to Melor Stonehoof in Thunder Bluff to turn in the quest.
