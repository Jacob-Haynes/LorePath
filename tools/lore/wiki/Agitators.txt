== Objectives ==
Slay 6 Roiling Winds and 7 Al'Aketh Converts in Thendal Grove.

Al'Aketh Convert slain (7)
Roiling Winds destroyed (6)


== Description ==
The Al'Aketh cult is a blight upon this island. These cultists are surely the ones responsible for agitating the simple wind sprites that reside here and turning them violent.
Regrettably, we'll need to disperse these roiling winds here before they harm someone. We also need to send a clear message to the cult that they are not welcome in the grove.
Will you lend me your aid, <class>?


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

50
250 XP
+50 reputation with Windshapers ( only)
+50 reputation with High Order ( only)


== Progress ==
Is it done?


== Completion ==
It pains me to shed shen'dorei blood here in the grove, but we do what we must.
