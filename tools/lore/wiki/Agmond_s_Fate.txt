This quest is part of the Ironband Wants You! quest chain.  This quest chain starts with  [37] Ironband Wants You!


== Objectives ==
Bring 4 Carved Stone Urns to Prospector Ironband in Loch Modan.

 [Carved Stone Urn] (4)


== Description ==
The last report I received from Agmond is troubling, more so now that he's dead! He mentioned that his diggers uncovered "ancient urns" at the dig site outside Uldaman. I fear that the urns he found at that site incited the troggs, just as did the idols I found here.
I need you to get those urns for our studies. Go to the Uldaman site, gather the urns, then return to me.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

 [Prospector Gloves]
You will also receive:

2850 XP (or 17 40 at level 70)
+250 reputation with Ironforge


== Progress ==
Did you find those urns, <name>?  We must find the link between them and the troggs!


== Completion ==
Well done!  With luck and diligence, we will find whatever link exists between the troggs and these ancient artifacts.  
You have done a great deed for the Prospectors, <name>.  Your efforts will not be forgotten.  And neither will Agmond.


== Notes ==
Although the quest text says "the dig site outside Uldaman", the urns are actually inside the Uldaman caves (just outside the instanced area). There are no urns on the dig site outside.
