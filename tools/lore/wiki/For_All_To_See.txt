== Description ==

<Thrall salutes you.>

== Rewards ==
You will be able to choose one of these rewards:

 [Dragonslayer's Signet]
 [Onyxia Tooth Pendant]
 [Onyxia Blood Talisman]
You will also receive:

9950 XP (or 60 at max level)
+200 reputation with Orgrimmar


== Completion ==
Choose your reward and let the celebration of your glorious victory begin.
