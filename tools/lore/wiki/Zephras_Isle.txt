Zephras Isle is the starting zone for the Skyborne in World of Warcraft: Forever. It is a flying island located in Skywall, the Elemental Plane of air.
The air elementals who used to live on the island with the Skyborne vanished a number of years ago, and now the magic sustaining the island is slowly fading. There are other islands in Skywall that the Skyborne also live on, named Anvilas and Siroccas, but Zephras Isle has lost contact with them since the elementals disappeared. As the other islands collapse under their feet, refugees are evacuating to Zephras, but even Zephras itself is starting to fall apart.


== Getting there ==
Playable Skyborne start on Zephras Isle in Thendal Village in the northwest. Once they finish the campaign, they will leave from the Valanaar Skydocks at the southern tip of the island. Alliance players will get dropped off at Dalaran in Alterac Mountains, while Horde players land at the Skywatcher Plateau in northwestern Mulgore.
All players, whether or not they've purchased the Skyborne Heroic Pack or other packs, can travel to Zephras from the airship landings in Dalaran or Mulgore.

  on the eastern Valanaar skydock ↔  at the northern end of Dalaran in Alterac Mountains
  on the western Valanaar skydock ↔  by Skywatcher Plateau in Mulgore
