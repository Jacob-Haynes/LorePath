This quest is part of the Lescovar Incident.


== Objectives ==
Speak with Baros Alexston in Stormwind. 


== Description ==
Ah, I almost forgot. Alexston came while you were away, he wished to speak with you. No doubt he wishes to report the goings-on of the Defias Brotherhood to the nobles.
A bit of advice, <name>: if anyone should ask, I would provide all details but that it was your hand that slew Lescovar. Trust me. I am... acquainted... with the ways of such things.
And as far as Trias goes, well, speak to no one of him... ever... if you can help it. 


== Rewards ==
625 XP (or 3 90 at level 70)
+25 reputation with Stormwind


== Completion ==
It's good to see you, <name>. I have heard that Lord Gregor Lescovar was found dead not long ago.
It would seem that you were successful in your mission. You have my gratitude that you have saved me, and many others, from VanCleef's revenge.
