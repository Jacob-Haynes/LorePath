Guile of the Raptor is the seventh and final step in the Hammerfall quest chain.


== Objectives ==
Speak with Tor'gan.


== Description ==
This... this power.... Is this the power of the shamans that Tor'gan tried to show me? I must say, it is interesting. Tell Tor'gan that he has convinced me... for the time being. I will listen to what he has to say.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

3700 XP
+100 or +250 reputation with Orgrimmar
 [Call of the Raptor]


== Completion ==
When the enchantment wears off, I will speak with Gor'mul. My thanks to you for your help in returning Gor'mul to the land of the living. I have no doubt he thanks you greatly as well.
