Stormwind Rendezvous is part of the Great Masquerade quest chain.


== Objectives ==
Travel to Stormwind City and venture to the city gates. Speak with Squire Rowe so that he may let Marshal Windsor know that you have arrived.


== Description ==
You are to meet with Windsor at the gates of Stormwind.
What Windsor is about to unveil will shake the very foundation of the kingdom!
Do not attempt to venture there alone, <name>. You must amass an army! Gather your compatriots. To arms! 


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

650 XP (or 3 90 at max level)


== Completion ==
Before we begin, I ask that you first gather an army. We face a dragon, after all! 


== Notes ==
You do have to speak to Squire Rowe to have Reginald Windsor show up at the gates, if he does not appear on his own.

Squire Rowe runs outside the gates of Stormwind and fires off a flare, then returns to his spot.
Squire Rowe says: The signal has been sent. He should be arriving shortly.
Reginald Windsor rides through the gates of Stormwind atop his steed, Mercutio. He then sends the horse running.
Reginald Windsor says: Yawww!
Reginald Windsor says: I knew you would come, <name>. It is good to see you again, friend.
If Marshal Windsor has already been summoned
