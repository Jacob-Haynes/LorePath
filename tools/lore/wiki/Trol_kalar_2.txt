Trol'kalar is the final step in the long Trol'kalar quest chain.


== Objectives ==
Deliver Trol'kalar to Zengu in Hammerfall. 

 [Sheathed Trol'kalar] (provided)


== Description ==
The light slowly fades, the sword Trol'kalar, held within a stone sheath has been released.


== Rewards ==

You will receive:

6000 XP
+150 or +350 reputation with Darkspear Trolls
