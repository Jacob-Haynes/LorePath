Shen'dralas is a contested zone in World of Warcraft: Forever, south-east of Desolace and between Feralas and Mulgore.

== Notes ==
Shen'dralas is placed roughly where Lake Dumont is located in Retail.
Highborne ruins can be found on Lake Dumont's northern shore, which may have been the inspiration for Shen'dralas.
