Return to Denalan is the 1st quest in the Return to Denalan quest chain.


== Objectives ==
Rellian Greenspyre wants you to speak with Denalan at Lake Al'Ameth.


== Description ==
I have just received word that  Denalan at Lake Al'Ameth has discovered what may be the cause of the tumors that plague the timberlings. Please speak with him, and tell him I sent you.
Make haste, <name>; he needs our help.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

80 XP (or 60 at max level)
+10 reputation with Darnassus


== Completion ==
Ah, so Rellian sent you to aid me. I am glad you arrived here so soon.
I have discovered something quite disturbing!


== Trivia ==
This quest was removed when Denalan moved next to Rellian Greenspyre in Wellspring Hovel, making this quest redundant, and came between the quests Tumors (now Mossy Tumors) and Oakenscowl.


== Patch changes ==
 Patch 4.0.3a (2010-11-23): Removed.
