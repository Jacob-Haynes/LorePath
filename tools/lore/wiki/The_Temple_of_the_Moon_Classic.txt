The Temple of the Moon is an optional breadcrumb quest that starts at the Temple Gardens in Darnassus.


== Objectives ==
Sister Aquinne wants you to speak with Priestess A'moora in the Temple of the Moon.


== Description ==
A once great creature of the forest is in need of help, <race>.
There is a difficult task that must be done.
Please seek out Priestess A'moora; she will explain things to you. Find her to the southeast of here, inside the Temple of the Moon.


== Rewards ==
You will receive:

85 XP
+10 reputation with Darnassus


== Completion ==
So, Sister Aquinne sent you?
