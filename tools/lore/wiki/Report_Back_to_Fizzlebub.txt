Report Back to Fizzlebub is a neutral quest from the Mirage Raceway in Thousand Needles. In this quest, players return to Booty Bay to let Crank Fizzlebub know how Kravel Koalbeard's scheme went.
Go to the Mirage Raceway in the Shimmering Flats of Thousand Needles, and talk to Kravel Koalbeard. Players must have completed either  [44] Get the Gnomes Drunk or  [44] Get the Goblins Drunk to receive this quest.


== Objectives ==
Bring the Fool's Stout Report to Crank Fizzlebub in Booty Bay. 

 [Fool's Stout Report] (provided)


== Description ==
That Fool's Stout worked great! I placed a bet on the next race, and... let's hope the crew you got drunk did a number on their car!
There's only one more task for you -- take this note back to Crank Fizzlebub in Booty Bay. He'll want to know of the successful test.
And hopefully we can get more of that Fool's Stout for future races!


== Rewards ==
