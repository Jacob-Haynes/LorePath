Rath'mael is the last boss in the Ruins of Lordaeron instance, found at the Lordaeron Graveyard.


== Abilities ==
 Fire Shield
 Flamestrike — Calls down a pillar of flame, burning all enemies in a selected area and inflicting additional damage every 2 sec. for 8 sec.
