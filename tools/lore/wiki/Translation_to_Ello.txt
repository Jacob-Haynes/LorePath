== Objectives ==
Bring the Translated Letter to Lord Ello Ebonlocke.

 [Translated Letter] (provided)


== Description ==
Oh my! If what is written on this note is true, then Abercrombie's done something...unspeakable.
You better bring the translated note back to Ello.
And do so quickly, for I fear a doom already lumbers toward our town... 


== Rewards ==
You will gain:

250 XP (or 1 50 at level 80)
300 reputation with Stormwind


== Progress ==
Ah, is the note translated?  Please, let me see it...


== Completion ==
<Ello reads the letter...and his face goes white.  He then looks at you...>
Although the aid you gave Abercrombie may have been motivated from kindness, your actions have doomed our town.
<Ello becomes lost in his thoughts...>


== Notes ==
Completing this quest sets in motion the monster known as Stitches, who approaches along the road from Raven Hill Cemetery to Darkshire, pausing along the way to kill the guards who oppose it. Only one Stitches NPC spawns at a time; if he is already spawned from a previous completion and has not died yet, he will not be spawned upon completion of the quest, but the guards still talk as if he was about to appear.
It seems that sometimes Stitches does not spawn, and no messages appear.
