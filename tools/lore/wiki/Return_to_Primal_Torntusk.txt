== Objectives ==
Elder Torntusk at Jintha'alor wants you to return to Primal Torntusk at Revantusk Village with news of his rescue.


== Description ==
Return to my mate, Primal Torntusk at Revantusk Village. Tell her that I am coming home! She is sure to reward you.
