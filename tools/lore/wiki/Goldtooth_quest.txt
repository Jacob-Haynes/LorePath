This quest concludes the Stonefield Necklace quest chain.


== Objectives ==
Bring Bernice's Necklace to "Auntie" Bernice Stonefield at the Stonefield Farm.

 [Bernice's Necklace]


== Description ==
I was playing near the Fargodeep Mine, and I think I dropped, er...I mean I saw, the old lady's necklace.  Don't ask me how it got there...it wasn't me!
Well anyway, I saw this big, gold-toothed kobold pick up the necklace and run back to his den above the mine.  Go find that kobold and you'll find the necklace, I swear!


== Rewards ==
You will be able to choose one of these rewards:

 [Lion-Stamped Gloves]
 [Uncle Stonefield's Pants]
 [Auntie's Oven Mitts]
You will also receive:

200 XP
+250 reputation with Stormwind


== Progress ==
Hello, <name>.  Have you found my necklace?


== Completion ==
Oh, you found it!  Thank you, thank you dear!
Here, take this.  It was my husband's and he always said it was lucky.  If only he didn't forget it on his last campaign! *sniff*


== Notes ==
Goldtooth can be found on a hill northeast of the mine.

 The subject of this section has been removed from World of Warcraft but is present in Classic.
In Classic, he is located within the mine on the lower level.
